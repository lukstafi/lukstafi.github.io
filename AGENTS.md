# Notes for agents

## Writing and editing essays (`notes/*.md`)

Follow Steven Pinker's *The Sense of Style* (classic style): the writer shows the reader something; the reader can see it too. Prose stays in the author's voice.

- Density means fewer words per idea, not more ideas per sentence. Cut recaps of companion essays, restated conclusions, and disclaimers against misreadings few readers would make. Do not merge sentences to save words.
- One move per sentence, given before new. Keep semicolons for true parallel pairs ("in the intact system, X; under playback, not-X"), not for chaining.
- Introduce material close to where it is used; repetition across distant sections is acceptable when it saves the reader a lookup.
- Cut phrasing that is unanchored or dangles (a "this" or "such" without a clear referent, a term used before it is introduced).
