# Typed genetic programming: research workspace

Revisit Łukasz Stafiniak's 2001–2005 programme in typed genetic programming,
generalization, and program synthesis. A focused literature survey now supports
an outline for a 25th-anniversary blog essay. Building our own experiments is
deferred.

## Archive

| Source | Date recorded in source | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MGP1.TXT](sources/MGP1.TXT) | October 2001 | Modular genetic programming, genealogical modules, maximal common schemata |
| [GENERA.TXT](sources/GENERA.TXT) | July 2002 | Mutation-compatible generalization; CSA algorithm drafts |

These originals were moved from `notes/private/` without changing their bytes.
[manifest.json](manifest.json) records the previous paths, sizes, and SHA-256
checksums of the four originals and three reading copies.

This directory is a public research archive tracked in the repository. The
original documents are historical drafts and thesis materials; the contemporary
research notes are provisional assessments, with their limitations recorded
alongside them. The archive is not yet linked from the website navigation or
included in its build scripts.

The anniversary is of the programme's October 2001 beginning. The thesis is
from 2005. The essay outline proposes an angle; the full draft remains to be
written. No claim of historical priority or new experimental result is made.
