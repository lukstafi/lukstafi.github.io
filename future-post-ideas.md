* **Which geometries can a rewriting universe earn?** What would let inhabitants of the Ruliad build two clocks, move them apart, and meet again to compare their readings? Chandrasekaran’s null-cone clock and the tensor geometry of a shared bit give cases where a law follows once the operations are specified. The open question from *The Standard of Motion* is which operations a rewrite rule can actually sustain. A bounded demon gives this a thermodynamic test. Which regularities in a memory can it use to lower the work of resetting it? Wolfram’s space demon poses the corresponding question for a signal that could outrun the apparent light cone. This also takes up the generative base from *What a Description Answers To*. When do two records describe one experiment, and when was the experiment performed twice?
