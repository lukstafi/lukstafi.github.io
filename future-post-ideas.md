* **Which geometries can a rewriting universe earn?** What would let inhabitants of the Ruliad build two clocks, move them apart, and meet again to compare their readings? Chandrasekaran’s null-cone clock and the tensor geometry of a shared bit give cases where a law follows once the operations are specified. The open question from *The Standard of Motion* is which operations a rewrite rule can actually sustain. A bounded demon gives this a thermodynamic test. Which regularities in a memory can it use to lower the work of resetting it? Wolfram’s space demon poses the corresponding question for a signal that could outrun the apparent light cone. This also takes up the generative base from *What a Description Answers To*. When do two records describe one experiment, and when was the experiment performed twice? How does the branchial divergence of rewrite histories give the relative phase observed when two arms of an experiment recombine? [Research note](notes/research/observers-and-the-ruliad-physics.md)
* **Observers and minds in the Ruliad.** What can a bounded observer know about its place in an irreducible whole? Take up the epistemic side of the demon and pseudoentropy material from *Before the Search Settles*. What odds should the observer give when it knows the generating rule but cannot run it in time? The PPA anthropics post (LessWrong, Oct 2026) raises the question of which observer one should expect to be. This strand awaits its technical and infinity posts. The monastery gives the question of participation a test. If the members stopped correcting one another, what capacity would the whole lose? Use residual integration to ask what “participation in the mind of God” could mean at the largest scale.
* Interpretability synthesis. Theses: "polysemantic" conflates polysemy with homonymy; the training regime decides between clean neurons and superposition; mapping a network is not certifying it. Test case: a neuron basis is identifiable only up to a linear map, and training can break the symmetry, so a privileged basis can be real because a process selected it.
