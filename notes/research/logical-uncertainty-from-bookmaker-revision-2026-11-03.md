# Logical uncertainty essay: points from the bookmaker essay's revision (3 Nov 2026)

Companion to `logical-uncertainty-essay-outline.md`; section numbers refer to that outline.

*The Bookmaker Is a Learner* gained a box "Hedging and exploring" (experts vs bandits, Exp3, the cold learner; the bookmaker buys information instead of exploring), a box "Mirror descent" (shares and prices as dual coordinates; the KL-regularized step, natural policy gradient and an LMSR trade as one step; stochastic mirror descent after Frongillo, Della Penna and Reid; probabilities near zero move slowly, and a market escapes this because a trader who believes in a cheap outcome profits most by buying it), and a liquidity paragraph naming the temperature tradeoff.

- **§2 accuracy.** Logical induction's MarketMaker is not a cost-function bookmaker: no liquidity $b$, no $b\log n$ subsidy, no temperature. It computes an approximate fixed point at which the combined trader's holdings are nearly worthless, a clearing price, closer to the Eisenberg–Gale box of the bookmaker essay. Say which of the bookmaker essay's three ways to settle a bet it resembles, rather than "told in the bookmaker essay's vocabulary". Check against the paper's MarketMaker definition. The temperature tradeoff has no direct counterpart; the learning-rate role falls to the trader weights and budgets.
- **§6 settlement scale, a second question.** Besides "is the check independent of the beliefs?", ask "does anything have an incentive to bid up what the learner has priced near zero?" OPST learns on-policy, which is bandit feedback: sharpening drives minority answers toward zero, they are then rarely sampled, and the consensus gate discards exactly the questions where alternatives are near zero. A logical inductor is in Hedge's position: every trade is evaluated against what is proved, and non-dogmatism means a cheap true sentence gets bought. AlphaZero sits between: search explores (PUCT is a bandit rule per node), and the network learns from full visit distributions, the "research before trading" pattern.
- **§6, one step with three gradient sources.** The AlphaZero → MuZero → OPST scale can be read as one KL-proximal improvement step fed different estimates: exact outcomes, a possibly wrong model, self-agreement. A sentence or two; do not claim OPST is literally mirror descent.
- **§3 "wealth as memory".** The bookmaker essay's closing says that for traders who stake a fixed fraction of wealth, the reweighting is multiplicative, as in Hedge. If Beygelzimer, Langford and Pennock 2012 (Kelly bettors) is that update exactly (verify), the Hedge reading belongs to logical induction's named precedent rather than to logical induction.
- **§3 "one shared state (unity)".** The bookmaker essay says separate binary markets are coupled only through an arbitrageur's head, and that arbitrageurs draw a market's boundary. A logical inductor prices sentences atomically, but its traders are internal and the fixed point is computed jointly, so the coupling runs through its own state; its boundary is the class of efficient traders. One sentence.
- **§5 contrast (optional).** Unmarking in adaptive logics restores a conclusion at once; an incremental learner that drove a hypothesis near zero recovers slowly.
- **Small fixes.** §5 "the parlay dealers presume independence" is firmer than the bookmaker essay, which reports it as Oddpool's guess. §6 "the EMA rate as liquidity": β = 1 is the shallow market, where "one trade can carry its price far from any consensus". §5 "$w\to\infty$ recovers Minimal Abnormality" should say minimal by cardinality, which coincides with Minimal Abnormality only in *Presumed Normal*'s example.

## Landed in the draft (6 Nov 2026)

All of the points above are now in the essay, including the fixes to Minimal Abnormality and the parlay dealers. Checks made on the way: Beygelzimer, Langford and Pennock 2012 is the Bayes update exactly for full Kelly bettors (its abstract: "the market price reacts exactly as if updating according to Bayes' Law"; section 5.1, "Wealth redistributed according to Bayes' Law"), with an auctioneer "taking no profit and absorbing no loss". Fractional Kelly gives a time-discounted frequency, so the essay claims only the full-Kelly case. MarketMaker's bound is $W(T_n)\le 2^{-n}$ (Def./Prop. 5.1.2). The consensus gate drops a sample only when all $G$ rollouts agree; OPST does not measure the diversity of answers over training, so the sharpening claim is stated as the structure of the loop.
