# Which geometries can a rewriting universe earn?

Research for a possible essay on the Ruliad, 27 November 2026.

The [existing essay idea](../../future-post-ideas.md) starts with a bounded observer inside an irreducible whole. Its questions concern knowledge, anthropics and participation in a larger mind. The two preprints suggest an independent essay about how to recognize a physical law among all the structures the Ruliad contains. A rewriting system can encode a laboratory experiment. What would it take for inhabitants of that system to discover the law by doing the experiment themselves?

The proposal is to start with operations the inhabitants can repeat. Can they prepare a second copy, transport a signal, join two experiments along a common boundary, or compare readings after a change of frame? Then ask which numerical relations every consistent assignment to those operations must obey. The tensor paper supplies one exact example and one obstruction. The null-cone paper supplies a gravitational example of what agreement across a boundary requires. The moving twin supplies a demand on dynamics that both examples leave open. [Wolfram on observers in the Ruliad](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2026/02/what-ultimately-is-there-metaphysics-and-the-ruliad/)

## Open with two clocks

Imagine two laboratories keeping records of adjoining pieces of a fluctuating spacetime. A direction called “outward” in one points inward in the other. Each lab is free to label its own boundary. What must they agree on to describe one experiment?

Venkatesa Chandrasekaran asks a version of this question in a proposed quantization of four-dimensional vacuum gravity. A finite causal diamond has a corner where its two null boundaries meet. A frame attached to the corner tells each lab where to evaluate a field, even across geometries in which the same coordinate labels would land it elsewhere. When the labs join their regions, the induced geometry must match and the outward charges cancel. A simultaneous shift of the two boost frames is gauge. Their *relative* boost survives and is conjugate to the corner area density in gravitational units. The model gives precise content to the idea that two descriptions can refer to one physical event. [Chandrasekaran, §§3.5–3.6, especially Eqs. 3.63–3.66](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S3.SS6)

There is an actual reading to compare. Follow a pencil of light rays from an initial cut to a prescribed area element at the corner. In the classical construction, shear focuses the rays. With the same initial area and expansion, a sheared pencil takes a larger normalized affine interval to reach that area element. A mere relabeling leaves the reading alone. Sending gravitational radiation through the pencil can change it. The clock reads geometry along null rays. A material clock carried by a traveler would instead record proper time on a timelike path. [Chandrasekaran, §3.2, Eqs. 3.25–3.28 and Fig. 9](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S3.SS2)

This picks up the moving twin in *The Standard of Motion*. A boost of the frames at a corner is already meaningful in vacuum gravity. For a glider, the harder question is whether the rule can make a second glider with a slightly different velocity. The new essay could ask a question between those two. Can an observer made by a rewrite rule carry a record from one encounter to the next and recognize the same signal against that record? Describe the same preparation and readout on two foliations of one causal graph. The record at the readout event should agree. Then change the physical preparation, keeping the calibration fixed, and look for a different reading. A test on moving observers would also have to exhibit the carrier at the two velocities and recover the predicted relation between its readings. These are proposed tests for a rule. Chandrasekaran starts with continuum gravitational dynamics. [The Standard of Motion, “A computational physics must earn its first law”](../the-standard-of-motion.md#a-computational-physics-must-earn-its-first-law)

## What a bounded observer could check

Chandrasekaran gives “bounded” an instructive control case. In his two-dimensional Jackiw–Teitelboim example, infinitely many mutually orthogonal states of a bounded diamond can give the same clock statistics. In his four-dimensional proposal, he argues that a macroscopic diamond subject to an upper bound on the clock reading has a finite-dimensional physical Hilbert space. Shear now affects the clock, and the argument for finite dimension also uses consistency between cut and uncut amplitudes. These are different kinds of limit. One instrument can miss a distinction available to another. If the entire physical state space has dimension $D$, even an ideal single-shot measurement can perfectly distinguish at most $D$ mutually orthogonal records. The time available to identify a record imposes a further limit on a laboratory. [Chandrasekaran, §§2.2 and 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S5)

The null-cone proposal offers a benchmark for the recombination of amplitudes. The inner product used to glue two pieces of an experiment also governs interference, and Eq. 4.19 explicitly uses the Born rule. Gorard gives phase a geometric interpretation in the Wolfram model. The path-integral phase factor encodes the dispersion angle of a bundle of geodesics propagating through the multiway graph. An interferometer gives that proposal a concrete test. Prepare a qubit with a Hadamard gate, change the phase along one arm, then recombine with a second Hadamard. The two arms belong to the laboratory description. Each may contain many rewrite histories. For a chosen rule and preparation, can one read the relative phase from their branchial geometry and recover the change in the detector probabilities as the setting varies? [Gorard, §3.3, Eq. 124](https://content.wolfram.com/sites/13/2020/07/29-2-2.pdf#page=43) · [Chandrasekaran, §4.2, Eqs. 4.18–4.19](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS2)

## What follows from the ability to share a record?

Give three observers one common binary label. Now give each *pair* its own independent binary label. Draw the labs at the same three corners in both experiments. In the first arrangement, learning one label settles what both other observers hold. In the second, learning what Alice shares with Bob leaves what Bob shares with Carol open.

OpenAI's tensor preprint assigns a price to each arrangement. A tensor here specifies which labels occur together. Local linear processing can only lower its spectral invariant, independent tensor products multiply the invariant, and a direct sum adds the invariants. Taking a scaled logarithm makes the prices of independent resources add. The paper constructs compatible prices on real Hilbert-space placements over any coefficient field. Once every two-site binary wire costs its Euclidean length throughout a flat, the price for a common binary label is forced to be the normalized mean width of the sites' convex hull. On our triangle of perimeter $P$, the common label costs $P/2$. The three independent wires cost $P$. The theorem concerns a resource for realizing tensors. To read a price as elapsed time, work, or a number of updates would require a model connecting those quantities. [OpenAI, Theorems 1.1, 1.2, 5.3 and Fig. 1](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Euclidean-geometry-in-the-asymptotic-spectrum-of-tensors-November-24-2026/paper.pdf)

This is a small case where one can say exactly what “observers like us must agree” amounts to. There is freedom in choosing the spectral invariant. Once it satisfies the algebra and the wire calibration, every such choice agrees on the common label. The existence of Euclidean flats of every finite dimension is itself a result. Selecting three spatial dimensions, a Lorentzian causal structure, or an observer capable of implementing the operations would require further arguments. The Ruliad essay could ask which part of the wire calibration follows from the observers' means of exchanging records. That would turn an appeal to their boundedness into a question with identifiable premises.

## A four-state calculation for the quantum sidebar

There is a tempting shortcut from tensor geometry to the branchial geometry of the published exposition. Here is an obstruction and a constructive version. Both are deductions from the tensor paper and elementary qubit geometry.

1. Take the four rays represented by $(|0\rangle+i^k|1\rangle)/\sqrt 2$, in order $k=0,1,2,3$. With the convention $d_{\rm FS}(a,b)=\arccos|\langle a|b\rangle|$, neighbors are $\pi/4$ apart and opposites are $\pi/2$ apart.
2. Spectral wire distances satisfy Eq. 4.5 of the paper. The sum of the two squared diagonals is at most the sum of the four squared sides. Our four rays give $\pi^2/2$ on the left and $\pi^2/4$ on the right. No uniform rescaling fixes the inequality. Even a limit of direct wire distances, with these six distances converging at one common scale, would obey it. This rules out that particular identification with the global Fubini–Study distance. Gorard's branchial graph distances have a different construction. [OpenAI, Thm. 4.3 and Eq. 4.5](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Euclidean-geometry-in-the-asymptotic-spectrum-of-tensors-November-24-2026/paper.pdf) · [Gorard, §3.2, especially Eqs. 95–98](https://content.wolfram.com/sites/13/2020/07/29-2-2.pdf)
3. Place each ray at $P_a/\sqrt 2$, where $P_a=|a\rangle\langle a|$, in the **real** Hilbert space of Hermitian matrices with the Hilbert–Schmidt inner product. By Theorem 1.1 its binary wire to $b$ costs $\|P_a-P_b\|_{\rm HS}/\sqrt 2=\sqrt{1-|\langle a|b\rangle|^2}=\sin d_{\rm FS}(a,b)$. These are chord lengths. For the same four states both sides of the inequality now equal 2.
4. An observer who can prepare only neighboring states could measure short chords and add them along a path. Divide the equatorial arc between opposite states into $n$ equal steps. Its price summed over the steps is $n\sin(\pi/(2n))$, which tends to $\pi/2$. The direct wire still costs 1. On the pure-state manifold the intrinsic path metric recovers the Fubini–Study angle because $\sin d/d\to1$ for short steps. The restriction to pure states matters. The ambient flat also permits straight shortcuts through points outside that manifold.

A rewrite model could try to recover both the allowed preparations and a rule for which short steps an observer can make. The calculation already assumes the qubit rays and their inner product. Its contribution is a pair of measurements with different numerical answers, and a precise account of which one can approach the quantum distance.

## Where the physics essay could end

Return to the moving twin. A common bit can be shared along a straight segment at a price equal to the distance between its ends. Adding another copy of the label midway costs nothing extra in that invariant (Proposition 5.2). Now ask the inhabitant to send a clock down the segment. Which sequence of rewrites transports it? Does it keep ticking on the way? Can the same rule carry a slightly faster copy? The price of the end-to-end correlation is already determined. Every answer about the traveling clock still depends on the dynamics. That difference gives the physics essay an ending of its own. A theory that contains every computation has room for every simulated clock. A theory of our clocks owes us the operations by which two of them can meet and compare what they have recorded. [OpenAI, Prop. 5.2](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Euclidean-geometry-in-the-asymptotic-spectrum-of-tensors-November-24-2026/paper.pdf) · [The Standard of Motion](../the-standard-of-motion.md)

## What the lowest description answers to

[*What a Description Answers To*](../what-a-description-answers-to.md#counterfactuals-come-with-descriptions) makes a distinction easy to lose when a rewrite is pictured as an instruction on a page. An occurrence has its origin in the occurrences that produced it. A rule groups actual productions under a type and says what would happen on another input. Even the lowest rule we could discover does that second job. The essay calls the generation it answers to a *base*. It also says explicitly where its test for real patterns stops. An experimenter can intervene on a typed system. At the bottom, the evidence consists of actual productions recurring across different configurations.

Suppose a source emits two signals. Each triggers its own detector, and a later comparison uses both records. Production orders the emission before each detection and both detections before the comparison. It leaves the detections unordered with respect to one another. A foliation groups events into successive slices consistent with those dependencies. Changing the slicing adds no detection. To perform the experiment twice, the source can emit a second pair and the detectors can make fresh records. The second emission can even be triggered by the first comparison. The recurrence then has a witness inside the causal history. Describing production in this way requires a fact about which occurrences yielded which others. It requires no further fact about which independent event the universe executed first. Multiway evolution also includes alternative rewrites, so this argument about reordering independent events applies only where the same causal history is being described. [Wolfram, “Foliations and Coordinates on Causal Graphs”](https://www.wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/the-updating-process-for-string-substitution-systems/foliations-and-coordinates-on-causal-graphs/) · [“Bases, and where we are”](../what-a-description-answers-to.md#bases-and-where-we-are)

### Which recurrences can ground a law?

Chandrasekaran's gluing construction suggests a sharper test for the essay's requirement of stability across a shift of base. Give each candidate implementation a preparation, a way of joining it to a second apparatus, and an output measurement. A translation that matches the first apparatus's readings can still fail when the second apparatus is attached. In the qubit example above, a direct path measurement gives equal odds with either phase setting. In a fresh run, put a recombiner in place of that measurement. The two settings now give opposite, certain outputs. An apparatus that discards relative phase can pass the first test and fail the second. If the family includes recombination, the map must preserve its effects too. Chandrasekaran's construction imposes a related consistency requirement when a quantum region is cut and rejoined. [“The criterion”](../what-a-description-answers-to.md#the-criterion) · [Chandrasekaran, §4.2, Eq. 4.19](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1#S4.SS2)

This clarifies what a derivation from repetition would owe. There would have to be actual productions of joined preparations, detectors and records in the region, enough to ground a common typing across those arrangements. The base can remain free of primitive “woulds.” Judgments about what would happen when the next apparatus is attached already employ that typing. Stability across implementations then asks for the *same* way of identifying the preparations and their continuations, to the accuracy accessible in the regime. Finding a translation afresh after seeing each final record would miss the point. The published essay already gives the general criterion. The recombiner gives it a failure one could see.

The tensor example supplies the positive counterpart. Every compatible price calibrated on the wires of an entire Euclidean flat assigns the common bit on a triangle half its perimeter. The necessity of that result belongs to the described level. A substrate could earn its application by repeatedly realizing the calibrated wires and the tensor operations with the required effects. The proof says what follows for *any* realization meeting those conditions. Exhibiting one realization and showing that the conditions survive a shift of implementation are two further tasks. This is a way to make part of a physical law necessary at its level while keeping open the essay's empirical wager about where the law is realized. [OpenAI, Thm. 5.3](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Euclidean-geometry-in-the-asymptotic-spectrum-of-tensors-November-24-2026/paper.pdf) · [“A thesis, with a hostage”](../what-a-description-answers-to.md#a-thesis-with-a-hostage)

### What can count toward a chance?

Counting histories retains their multiplicity. Branchial divergence describes how neighboring histories spread through the multiway system. For the two equal arms of the ideal interferometer the standard quantum prediction is $p(0)=|(1+e^{i\phi})/2|^2$. At relative phase $\pi$, the phase factor is $-1$ and the amplitude at detector 0 vanishes. Every history count remains nonnegative. The two arms in this calculation give no grounds for assuming equal microscopic path counts across settings. Gorard's geometric interpretation suggests comparing the multiway evolutions generated by the two preparations and extracting the relative phase before predicting the readout. [Gorard, §3.3, Eq. 124](https://content.wolfram.com/sites/13/2020/07/29-2-2.pdf#page=43)

For the metaphysics, occurrences can remain individually unweighted while phase belongs to a description of their relations. A list of counts can omit those relations even when it counts every occurrence correctly. The question for the proposed essay is how the geometry of generation yields the observed frequencies across preparations. A measure over realizations elsewhere in the Ruliad would answer a further question. [“A thesis, with a hostage”](../what-a-description-answers-to.md#a-thesis-with-a-hostage)

That leaves a choice for the new essay. It can retain primitive production and present these examples as tests on the first physical description. Or it can ask what a rival who takes the whole algebra of admissible experiments as primitive would lose. The rival gets the same laboratory predictions if the algebra is the same. What the production postulate adds is a distinction between an experiment being specified, its being carried out, and its being carried out twice. Whether that distinction earns its keep is a metaphysical question. The shared-bit theorem and the gluing construction bring it into focus without deciding it.

## A demon with a work meter

Suppose a short, uniformly sampled seed is expanded into a long tape. Compare that ensemble with a tape of fresh fair bits. If the generator is secure against a specified class of tests, both ensembles pass those tests with nearly the same probabilities. The generated tapes have at most the seed length in bits of Shannon entropy. Their apparent randomness to the bounded tests can be much larger. This is a clean example of pseudoentropy. Bennett’s tape of digits of π makes the thermodynamic intuition vivid, though a fixed known string and a sampled ensemble are different examples. [Barak, Shaltiel and Wigderson, *Computational Analogues of Entropy*](https://www.boazbarak.org/Papers/compent.pdf) · [Bennett, *The Thermodynamics of Computation*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02084158)

Put a work meter on the demon. Give it the tape but withhold the seed. If its reset protocol and meter together implement an allowed test, a reliably detectable difference in reset work between the ensembles would itself distinguish them. The reduction is simple: run the protocol and report whether the work crossed a chosen threshold. This constrains the work statistics one can use to tell the sources apart. Calculating an actual reset cost takes a physical model of the memory, bath, controls and permitted error. Munson and colleagues supply a quantum example. For a degenerate memory in their gate-limited erasure model, a quantity they call complexity entropy characterizes the work needed to reset it. This gives the physics essay a measured consequence of bounding a controller. Cryptographic pseudoentropy and their complexity entropy have separate definitions. [Munson et al., *Complexity-Constrained Quantum Thermodynamics*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.6.010346)

Apply the same question to the space demon in *The Standard of Motion*. Can an apparatus built under the rewrite rule find, prepare and use a fast path to transmit a freely chosen bit before the ordinary signal arrives? The decisive resource budget includes setting up the channel, and a reusable channel can spread that cost over many messages. An effective speed limit would need a bound covering the protocols available to inhabitants. There is no deduction of such a bound from cryptographic pseudoentropy. The common move is to specify the apparatus and read out a physical consequence, in joules in one experiment and in arrival times in the other. [The Standard of Motion](../the-standard-of-motion.md) · [The Demon Is a Learner](../the-demon-is-a-learner.md)

## Possible contact with the essay on bounded observers

A demon observing a digit stream might know how to predict the next digit cheaply or might have to treat it as a fresh uncertainty. Give two demons the same stream and different time budgets. Ask which tests each can perform and how its predictions change. That is where the projected essay's discussion of pseudoentropy belongs. The tensor price above comes from a law of composition. Assigning the demons different budgets requires separate operational choices. Anthropics enters at a further step, when the essay asks which of the observers able to make such records one should expect to be. The physics examples supply finite experiments for that question. They leave the choice of measure and the planned treatment of infinity open. [Logical-uncertainty outline, material reserved for the observers essay](logical-uncertainty-essay-outline.md) · [Future essay idea](../../future-post-ideas.md)

A second possible connection runs through the three labs. A beacon could have sent the same bit to three receivers that never speak to one another. Or three members of a monastery could arrive at a common decision by correcting one another, then use it to change what each does tomorrow. One can code the final agreement in both cases by the same shared label. To learn what the monastery does as a whole, interrupt a channel *during* deliberation and see which corrections and subsequent actions disappear. Repeat for the partition that loses the least organization. That is the residual-integration question. One can participate in a common record through a single broadcast. The claim of participation in a larger mind needs an account of what the parts accomplish together over time. Scaling that question to the Ruliad would require specifying its parts, their interactions, and a meaningful intervention on their coupling. [A Mind and One Mind, on residual integration](../a-mind-and-one-mind.md)

A reason to bring the essays together would be a worked case in which changing an observer's resources changes which preparations, paths, or comparisons it can realize, and this changes the effective law it can infer. The short-chord calculation suggests such a case, provided the restriction on available paths can be derived from an observer and a rule. At present that restriction is supplied in the example. The physics essay can develop the triangle, the qubit and the moving clock on their own. The bounded-observer essay can pursue the demon, anthropics and the monastery on its own schedule.
