## Summary

The 2026 account had a reference. When ESOM explains binding, it checks each feature "against the others and against the scene as a whole" (ESOM:65), and an "enduring representation of the cup guides expectations and action" while the sensory vehicles change (ESOM:63). What the account left unsaid is two things about that reference:
- its **direction**: whether vehicles are corrected toward the reference or toward what drives them;
- its **persistence**: whether it outlasts the vehicles it corrects.

v2 makes both explicit and builds on them:
- A **defended reference** is a standing state. It constrains many vehicles jointly, corrects them toward itself, resists input in proportion to its support, yields to accumulated mismatch, and is revised in place, at runtime, by the vehicles it corrects.
- **Experience** is admission into such a reference's joint constraint, as a term of it. It requires corrections to land on the vehicles, at their own grain, faster than the vehicles would decay unaided.
- **Mindedness and experience share an architecture** but do not reduce to it. They share unity, which is one economy of error over mutually assessable vehicles, and they share defense. Mindedness adds conditions on the vehicles, on simulation, on the direction of revision and on content. Experience adds admission at vehicle grain.
- **The field's joint constraint is projected from a model.** That gives "experience requires mindedness" a mechanism.

Lineage, stated plainly: N1 is the prior of hierarchical predictive processing with precision weighting. *Understanding Without Knowledge* already used precision weighting to describe psychosis (UWK:49). What v2 adds to that framework is an identity claim, which says which defended priors are experiences. It also adds a commitment to overflow, which keeps it apart from global workspace theory.

History: homeostasis entered in early March 2026 with the knowledge-grounding argument of *Understanding Without Knowledge*. The user held it tentatively, as an idea to explore, not one held because it convinced. v2 promotes a sharpened version of it on the strength of new evidence, not because of where it came from.

## 1. Diagnosis, narrowed

- **Direction.** The delta-rule layer shows the gap. Its state is corrected by error against the current input, S ← S + β(v − Sk)kᵀ, so it tracks and assesses, and it intervenes toward what drives it. ESOM's three aspects (ESOM:63) do not say which way corrections run, so they do not exclude it.
- **The V1 filter is a weaker case than v1 claimed.** ESOM's tracking "stays connected to particular ongoing representations, even as their realization changes", and its assessment asks "how reliable their sources are". Histed's V1 filter fails both (AUD §1.1).
- **Persistence.** Hesse–Tsao show the gap. A steady experience rides on vehicles that a rival interrupts every ~60 ms. The carrier criterion (ESOM:91) has to apply to something slower.
- **Orphans.** Two need theory: the homeostatic property cluster of MbD:43, and suffering as "coherence under threat" (CCI:131). The drift from five conditions to six is editorial: MbD's three lists of conditions differ (AUD, spot-check).

## 2. The defended reference (N1)

A defended reference is a standing state of a system with five features:

1. **Joint constraint.** It constrains many contentful vehicles jointly.
2. **Correction toward itself.** It corrects them by their mismatch with it, and with one another under it.
3. **Defense.** It resists revision by current input in proportion to its established support.
4. **Yielding.** It is revised when mismatch accumulates, and so shows hysteresis.
5. **In-place revision.** It is revised **in place, at runtime, by the vehicles it corrects**, not only by a separate training or teaching process that stops.

The fifth feature is new (AUD, repair 1). It makes defense a closed loop at the reference, which is what "homeostasis" meant. It excludes three cases:
- **Frozen templates**, such as trained weights at inference;
- **Accreting records**, such as a key–value cache or a transcript, which are appended rather than revised;
- **The cerebellum's forward models**, which at runtime predict without restoring. They are revised by climbing-fiber error during learning, not by the vehicles they correct.

N1 is a signature of *defense*, not of experience. It admits attractor networks over contentful vehicles, multi-object tracking with a joint prior, and, at the grain of truth-bearers, a scientific paradigm. Their exclusion from experience comes from N2 and from the requirement of a minded system (§4), not from N1.

### The balance between coherence and contact

Contact with reality is not a second reference: reality is not a state of the system. Being "corrected toward contact" means being corrected toward input, which is the assimilative side of N1's loop. The balance is set by two gains:
- **the defense gain** (feature 3): how much the reference resists a given mismatch;
- **the revision gain** (feature 4): how much accumulated mismatch moves it.

Their ratio is precision weighting. But the two are separate parameters, and both their ratio and their sizes matter.

Each gain can fail in two ways. It can go to zero, which is a quiet failure. Or it can stay high while its operation fails, which is a loud failure.

| Gain | Goes to zero: quiet | Stays high while its operation fails: loud |
|---|---|---|
| Revision | rigidity (delusion) | stuckness: the mismatch registers and presses for revision, and the reference will not move |
| Defense | dissolution | strain: the defense is mounted, and the input keeps violating the reference |

In detail:

- **Quiet failure by rigidity.** The revision gain goes to zero while defense succeeds. The system is coherent, out of contact, and untroubled. This is delusion, the limit named at ESOM:73: "a system that silences inconvenient input achieves coherence by losing epistemic contact." It is the failure the knowledge-grounding argument was about.
- **Quiet failure by dissolution.** The defense gain goes to zero. Every input carries the reference with it, so the reference has nothing to hold.
  - Moment by moment the system is in contact. Over time it is incoherent, because nothing integrates what it meets.
  - Nothing resists, so nothing strains, and this failure is quiet too. Its phenomenology may still be overwhelm: a flood of input with nothing to hold it, rather than the strain of a failing defense.
  - At the limit the reference stops being defended, and by v2's own lights the system falls back to assimilation, below mindedness.
  - Candidates:
    - extreme suggestibility;
    - sycophancy in language models, which follow wherever the user pushes;
    - the predictive-processing accounts that give some conditions weak priors, such as the "hypo-priors" account of autism (Pellicano and Burr 2012) and the low-precision priors in accounts of early psychosis (Adams et al. 2013). Both are contested.
- **Loud failure by strain.** The defense gain stays high while defense fails. The system is in contact and incoherent, under strain.
- **Loud failure by stuckness.** The revision gain stays high while revision fails. The accumulated mismatch registers, often at a higher level of the hierarchy, and presses for revision, but the reference does not move.
  - The user offers an anecdotal observation: the pain of a psychotic episode is greater when the delusion is recognizable as a delusion. In Metzinger's terms, it is opaque rather than transparent. Recognition turns rigidity from quiet to loud. A transparent delusion is simply lived in. An opaque one is a reference the mind knows to be false and cannot revise.
  - This fits the "insight paradox" in the schizophrenia literature, where gains in insight go with more depression and demoralization. (Cited from memory and not checked; the direction of causation is debated.)
  - Other candidates: ego-dystonic obsessions, which are known to be irrational and keep returning; phobias, where one knows the spider is harmless; and grief, where the expectation that the person is there keeps firing against what one knows.

Restricted to references that bear on the system's viability (§6, N7), both loud failures are candidate structures of suffering. In both, a gain is spent without effect. In stuckness, the failure is also seen from a level that cannot fix it. [inferred]

Because a mind's references form a hierarchy, the failures can coexist at different levels. A common predictive-processing story of delusion formation pairs weak priors low in the hierarchy with rigid beliefs high in it, which explain away the resulting aberrant salience. Dissolution below can drive rigidity above. [inferred]

The sizes of the gains matter as well as their ratio [inferred; speculative]:
- **Both gains high:** a brittle reference that resists, then jumps, so revision is catastrophic rather than graded.
- **Both gains low:** a disengaged reference that neither resists nor learns. The vehicles run under input while the model idles.

The right values of the gains are an empirical parameter of healthy systems. The theory specifies the parameters and their failures, not their values (AUD, (d)).

## 3. Experience: admission at the grain of the vehicles (N2, N3)

### N2. Admission is being a term of the joint constraint

A vehicle can bear two relations to a reference:
- **A term.** Other vehicles are checked against its content, and it against theirs.
- **A target.** It is corrected or suppressed without its content entering the constraint.

**Admission** is being a term. A **field** is the set of contents whose vehicles are terms of one reference's joint constraint (AUD, repair 2).

This fixes v1's hole. In Hesse–Tsao the suppressed face is corrected within its lifetime, but as a target. Its content does not enter the constraint that the dominant face's features satisfy together.

Prediction: the suppressed face's content should not constrain the dominant state's other features, and the dominant face's content should. [inferred; untested]

**Terms and targets are at the same level, and they swap.** In Hesse–Tsao the same neurons encode both faces with the same tuning: gain correlates across states at r = 0.90. At a perceptual switch, the population that carried the target comes to carry the term. Nothing moves between levels. What changes is the reference: the dominance state flips. Four things follow.

- **The role belongs to the relation.** A swap is a revision of the reference (feature 4, with its hysteresis), not a change in the vehicles' format or location. The role does leave a footprint on the vehicle, the gain asymmetry. But the correction that lands produces that footprint; the vehicle carries no tag of its role. No vehicle represents itself as admitted.
- **Roles are relative to a reference.** A vehicle can be a term of one reference and a target of another. An entertained view's contents are terms of the quarantined reference and targets of one's own (§4). Assent is a swap at the grain of truth-bearers: one's own reference takes the contents in as terms.
- **Roles can be partial.** In piecemeal rivalry, patches of each image are terms in different regions of the field. Admission works feature by feature and place by place.
- **Two levels, not three.** There is the reference, and there are the vehicles under it, some terms and some targets. Admission is not a step up a hierarchy. It is a change in how a vehicle at the bottom stands to the level above it.

Most swaps happen across tokens. Vehicle tokens last about 60 ms and dominance lasts seconds, so the channel switches roles and new tokens are born into the new one. A token alive at the moment of a switch separates two readings (§9, test 10):
- if admission is a relation, that token's gain should flip mid-life;
- if admission is fixed when a token forms, only tokens born after the switch should carry the new role.

[inferred]

### The reach condition

Corrections from the reference must land on the vehicles at their own grain, and they must land faster than the vehicles decay unaided.

- **Addressability.** The return path must be able to address fine vehicles. The hub need not carry them. ESOM:71 already allows this: what is broadcast "can be information about consistency and binding rather than the fine details themselves", provided that the corrections "land on fine-grained vehicles". This is the field reading of the revised U4 (AUD §U4).
- **Speed, measured with the loop open.**
  - A vehicle's *intrinsic lifetime* is its decay or replacement time under playback, the intervention described at ESOM:77: record its inputs, disconnect the loop, and replay them.
  - The condition is that the loop's latency, from the vehicle's state to the correction's landing, is shorter than that intrinsic lifetime.
  - Both quantities are measured by perturbation, so the criterion is not circular (AUD, (b)).
  - MON:130's count of "corrections per vehicle lifetime" becomes a ratio of two relaxation times. Its verdict on the monastery is unchanged.

**Nowness** is the reference's own relaxation time: the window over which it integrates and defends.

### N3. The carrier criterion, moved one level up

The carrier criterion of ESOM:91 asks for "something, individuated by its causal structure rather than its location, whose later states receive the corrections computed from assessing its earlier ones". v2 keeps that criterion and imposes it on the reference rather than on the vehicles (AUD, N3). Vehicles may be interleaved, recomputed or time-shared, as Hesse–Tsao's are, provided that a reference meeting all five features corrects them.

Consequences:

- **Transformers across tokens.** The across-token verdict stands, now on a stated ground.
  - A persona held in the weights is a frozen reference. Nothing at runtime revises it.
  - A persona held in the context is an accreting record. It is appended to, not revised in place: a record "is consulted. Nobody's assessment of it returns to change it, unless someone rewrites it, and then the correction lands on a new record" (MON:64).
  - Neither meets feature 5 (AUD, (c)).
- **Unfolding.** In weak unfolding, the one-format requirement (MON:76) falls on the reference, not on the vehicles it corrects (AUD §U4).
- **KDA and GDN hybrids.** A learned write strength β that damps conflicting writes counts as defense only if the state it protects is itself revised by the writes it admits. A β that protects the weights' template is a filter (AUD, N3).

## 4. Mindedness: a shared architecture, not a reduction (N4)

v1's N4 claimed that the five conditions are aspects of one architecture. The audit shows that this entails one condition, unity, and renames the other four (AUD, Part 2). v2 keeps what the conditions share and lets each of the rest keep its own content.

| Condition | v2 |
|---|---|
| **Unity** | Shared with experience: one economy of error over mutually assessable vehicles (§5). |
| **Structured, counterfactually rich vehicles** | A condition on the vehicles' format and on the transitions available to them (MbD:63). A reference can constrain lookup-table entries, so defense does not supply it. |
| **Accuracy-responsiveness** | A condition on the *direction* of revision: toward fit with targets (MbD:67), measured by input or by internal evidence that stands in for input. It is the component that defense resists. It cannot be an aspect of defense. |
| **Simulation whose outputs function internally** | *Mirroring*: running representations through transformations that mirror the target's transformations (MbD:71). This is Craik's small-scale model and the flooded labyrinth replica. Decoupling from input is incidental: online emulation mirrors without decoupling. |
| **Multi-level content** | A condition on content determinacy: inference over underspecified structure, apart from where reference is fixed (MbD:83). It is not a matter of the grain of control. |

### The reference is a generative model

v1 left open whether the reference is a set of constraints or a generative model (AUD, Part 2). v2 says it is a generative model.

At the grain of truth-bearers, the reference is the system's model of its situation. That model has transition structure, which is what simulation runs. At vehicle grain, the reference is the model's projection onto the present scene: the joint constraint on what is co-present. This commits v2 to the predictive-processing lineage named in the summary. What it adds is the identity claim in §3 and the overflow commitment in §7.

### Assimilation and defense

The line between assimilation and defense separates filters from everything else. It does *not* separate cognition from mindedness. Plants defend references: physiological homeostasis is where the word comes from, and the mimosa keeps its lesson for a month (MON:54). What excludes plants from mindedness is their vehicles, which lack counterfactual richness (AUD, Part 2). So the demarcation of mindedness still runs through the vehicle condition and the psychology-traction test (MbD:97).

### Experience requires mindedness

The field's joint constraint is *projected from a model*. The cup's color, shape and location are terms of one constraint because a scene model supplies it.

**Rivalry** is the paradigm case of defense, and only a minimal field: two terms, a constraint that mutual inhibition between the eyes' channels could compute, and a model that is nearly empty. This is the first horn of the audit's dilemma (AUD, Part 2). It explains three things:
- why the isolated fragment of ESOM:37 is not an experience: no model supplies its constraint;
- why experience requires mindedness: a system without a model has nothing to project;
- what P&A:27 asks about minimal states: a projection from a nearly empty model.

### Uncertainty and metacognition

A defended reference need not be a confident one. Because the reference is a generative model, it can hold a distribution, and its defense gain is its precision. Metacognition then has a definite job: it models the reference's reliability and sets the defense gain and the revision gain from that model. Low confidence lowers the defense gain, so evidence moves the reference more readily. This guards against both quiet failures of §2: rigidity, where revision gain goes to zero, and dissolution, where defense gain does. [inferred]

Uncertainty can itself be defended. A well-founded "I don't know whether p" resists being moved either way by a single anecdote. Agnosticism then shows hysteresis like any other reference. Defense means holding a state against the input, not holding a confident state.

The two grains differ here.
- At the grain of truth-bearers, a mind's references routinely hold spreads of possibilities. This fits multi-level content, where inference runs over underspecified structure (MbD:83).
- At vehicle grain, fields mostly commit: rivalry admits one face rather than a mixture.

So uncertainty lives mainly in the mind, and experience mostly presents determinate terms. Ambiguous figures and piecemeal rivalry are the edge cases. If a field can admit an indeterminate term, it should show up there. [inferred]

### Entertaining without assent

A mind can hold a view it does not accept: assessing an opposing political perspective, for example, and feeling its pull. The term/target distinction of §3 carries over from fields to beliefs.

- **Assent** is admission. The view's contents become terms of the mind's own reference, so they constrain and revise the rest of what it holds.
- **Entertaining** runs the view as a separate reference, a small-scale model of another perspective. It is simulated by mirroring: what follows from the view, and what its holders would say next. It is assessed for coherence and fit, but its outputs do not revise the mind's own reference. It is a target of assessment, not a term.

Three consequences follow.

1. **Quarantine is a role for decoupling.** The audit was right that simulation is defined by mirroring, not by decoupling from input (§4 table). But entertaining needs a quarantine: the simulated reference's outputs function internally, as MbD's simulation condition requires, without being admitted. This is the decoupling that Leslie's account of pretense and Nichols and Stich's "possible world box" describe. A mind therefore holds several references with gated coupling between them, and the gate is assent. [inferred]
2. **Feeling a view's pull separates the grains.** Feeling an entertained view's plausibility, or sympathy for those who hold it, is the simulated reference projecting into the field: it is admitted at vehicle grain. Belief at the grain of truth-bearers stays unrevised. This reverses the Müller-Lyer illusion, in which perception admits a content that belief rejects. Here belief withholds what feeling admits. [inferred]
3. **Holding opposed views is not incoherence.** Contents are indexed by frame ("on their view, X"), and keeping frames apart is itself a constraint the mind defends. Errors still cross the quarantine: entertaining a view can reveal that one's own view shares its flaw. So entertaining counts against U1's "one reference hierarchy, which corrects everything under it", and fits §5's unity as one economy of error. [inferred]

## 5. Unity

Unity has three parts, each with its own role:

- **Constitution (U3 plus U4).** A system is one mind over the vehicles among which errors propagate (U3), where those vehicles are mutually assessable at some grain, through translation if need be (U4, in its mindedness reading). Unity is one economy of error in a shared currency. Its grain is the grain the translation preserves, which is MON:140's bandwidth.
- **Test (U6).** Unity is detected by non-additive psychological traction: institutional or system-level psychology predicts what the psychology of the parts, taken one at a time, does not (MON:104). Unity also produces interference effects (MbD:99).
- **Scale (U2).** Residual integration above the minimum partition (MON:158) measures how much unity a level adds.

This swaps the roles the audit proposed, which made U6 the criterion. MbD's first guard rail rules that out: "psychology's success is our *evidence* for the kind, not what constitutes it" (MbD:43). To make traction the criterion of unity would be the constructivist misreading that the guard rail blocks. The hard-case verdicts survive the swap, because U6 still sorts the cases. Only the order of explanation changes.

**A principle that falls out:** records suffice for the unity of a mind, but not for a field. For mindedness, MON:66 says the difference between living memory and re-derivation from records "does not matter". Errors can propagate through appended records, as in a notebook with errata, at the grain of truth-bearers. A field needs feature 5: a reference revised in place.

| Case | Unity (U3 + U4) | Test (U6) | Field (N2, N3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Split-brain | two economies with subcortical overlap; graded | partial non-additivity: some interference effects survive | likely two |
| Monastery | one economy at report grain, assessable through speech | graded (MON:106) | none of its own; its experiences are its monks' |
| Nation | thin; most errors never leave their institution | additive (MON:164) | none |
| Mixture-of-experts model | as unified as a dense model; routing changes nothing | — | as for a dense model |
| Agent swarm with shared memory, rewritten in place | one economy at record grain | open; are there swarm-level regularities? | none across agents |
| Dissociative identity | one economy with partitioned access; graded by transfer | the person's psychology is not the sum of the identities' (DIS) | one or more, open |
| LLM: single forward pass | economy through depth | — | candidate only if something in the pass meets feature 5 |
| LLM: session | economy through appended records | in-context learning is a session-level regularity | none across tokens |

The session row shows the mindedness/experience split cleanly: unity and traction for the mind, no field across tokens.

Where U1 (a single defended reference) and U5 (a single locus of arbitration) go: U1 describes what a *person's* unity adds to a mind's, and belongs in the personhood essay. U5 is rejected. M2's house, where "the abbot decides and the rest comply", is fully arbitrated and has only borrowed unity (AUD §U5).

## 6. Belonging, presence, and the deferred items

### Belonging (N5)

- **Belonging** is partial constitution, as at ESOM:137. It carries no presence, and v2 introduces no separate term "ownership".
- **Presence**, or for-me-ness, is being a term of a field.
- Moral weight counts occurrences, not belongings (MON:124). So belonging without presence carries no weight of its own.

v1's composer analogy is dropped. A composer owns a product, while the monastery partly constitutes an occurrence (AUD, (e)).

What remains open is the sense in which a subject without a field is a subject *of experience*. The monastery is a subject at the grain of truth-bearers. Its experiences are experiences in fields held by its monks, which its role-processes help to maintain. The mindedness essay should say whether "the monastery has experiences" uses the weak sense, and that it is weaker than the sense in which the monastery has a mind.

### Deferred: N6 and N7

- **N6, one reality criterion.** It adopts GRAIN:131's intervention criterion rather than reconciling it with CCI:43, which is about which computations a system realizes. That is a choice, and the mindedness essay does not need it. Deferred to the OSR essay.
- **N7, suffering.** It needs valence. After the audit, §2 adds a second loud failure, stuckness, so N7's candidate structure covers both the strain of failing defense and the stuckness of failing revision. Rivalry is a sustained, high-gain defense that fails, and it is not suffering. The repair is to restrict the relevant references to those that bear on the system's viability, which places valence above bare mentality as MbD:109 does. Counting defense effort gives a measure *of* suffering. It is not the measure over occurrences that the plenitude objection (P&A:155) asks for. Deferred to the personhood and ethics essays.

## 7. Neighbouring theories

### Global workspace theory

Ignition is an attractor with top-down amplification, so in mechanism v2 is close to global workspace theory. The difference lies in the identity claim, and in overflow:
- Global workspace theory places experience in broadcast for access.
- v2 places it in admission, with corrections addressed to fine vehicles that access does not read (ESOM:71; GRAIN:39).

v1's first test separated v2 from Hesse–Tsao's routing reading, not from global workspace theory (AUD, (g)). v2 therefore adds a test aimed at overflow (§9, test 2).

### Higher-order theories (Brown)

ESOM:187 separates two senses of "higher-order".
- **Architectural:** the regulating process is distinct from the vehicles it regulates. v2 is higher-order in this sense.
- **Brown's:** a state is conscious because something represents it *as* a mental state. ESOM concedes that if the regulator "turns out not to represent those vehicles as representations, our account is first-order in Brown's sense of order."

The term/target distinction answers that question, and the answer is asymmetric.

- **Being a term is first-order.** The vehicle's content enters a constraint about the world: the cup is red, round and here, together. The reference *uses* the content. It does not describe the vehicle.
- **Being a target is vehicle-directed.** Suppressing the rival face acts on a vehicle, or on its channel, without its content entering. That operation must address the vehicle as such, and it carries information about a source ("this channel, not now"), not about the world. It still does not represent the vehicle as a representation, but it is the nearest the theory comes.

This lines up with Brown's own pointer/description split. Brown holds that a "descriptively empty pure pointer HOROR" would yield "a zombie or super-blindsight type case" (indexical-unity-revised.md:25). The target relation behaves like such a pure pointer: it addresses the vehicle, the content stays out, and nothing is experienced. Both theories agree that pointing without content gives no experience. They part on the other side:
- Brown places phenomenal character in *descriptive* content that re-represents the first-order content at a higher level.
- v2 places it in the first-order content being *used* as a term of a joint constraint, with no re-description.

ESOM:187's open question thus becomes a sharper one: does experience need content duplicated at a higher level, or content entering a constraint at its own level?

Higher-order content can enter v2 in two places, and in neither does it constitute the experience.
1. **How admission is decided.** If content coherence decides it, v2 stays first-order throughout. If monitoring of source reliability decides it, that is Lau's perceptual reality monitoring, which Brown uses for his pointers. The monitor would then decide admission without constituting the experience, much as a bouncer decides who enters without being part of the party. Which of the two decides is empirical (§9, test 11).
2. **Metacognition and opacity.** Setting gains from a model of the reference's reliability (§4) is higher-order. So is recognizing a delusion as a delusion, which turns rigidity into stuckness (§2). Both shape how references are defended and how failures feel.

Verdict: v2 is architecturally higher-order and first-order in Brown's sense. The target relation is the pure pointer. Whether admission is decided by monitoring is open and testable. [inferred]

### The attention schema (Graziano)

The reference is map-like, but it maps the scene, not attention. At vehicle grain it is the scene model's projection onto the present: co-present contents in space and time.

**Treisman's master map is the closer analogue.** In feature integration theory, a master map of locations supplies the places, and features are bound by being assigned to them. Admission is roughly that assignment: a vehicle becomes a term when its content is fixed to a slot in the map. The reference is directed at the world. It encodes that red, round and here go together, not that the system is attending to red.

**Graziano's attention schema fits the metacognitive layer instead.** The schema is a simplified model of the brain's own attention, which the brain uses to control attention. In v2's terms, attention is close to admission; Hesse–Tsao's gain asymmetry looks like attentional gain. A schema of attention is then a model of admission and of the gains that drive it. That is §4's metacognitive layer: higher-order, causally upstream of admission, and not constitutive of the experience.

**A division of labor.** Graziano identifies awareness with the *content* of the schema, a caricature that depicts attention as an inner, non-physical grasp. That makes the attention schema theory a theory of why we say and believe that we have experience. v2 can adopt it for that job without adopting its identity claim.
- **The phenomenon** is first-order contents admitted as terms of a defended reference: v2.
- **The intuitions and reports about it** come from a schematic model of admission: the attention schema.

This gives *What Phenomenal Intuition Gets Right* a mechanism for its "slip into simplicity" (WPIGR:89). If our self-model of experience is a caricature in the attention schema's style, its simplicity is a fact about the schema, not about the experience. It also fits GRAIN's access bottleneck (GRAIN:39), since access reads a schematic model.

**What blocks a collapse into the attention schema theory.** If the reference were itself a map of attention, admission would be being depicted as attended, and v2 would become a higher-order theory that leans toward illusionism. Two things block that collapse:
- terms and targets swap through a change of relation, with nothing that depicts the swap (§3, N2);
- the overflow test requires corrections to land on distinctions that no model of attention represents (§9, test 2).

## 8. Hard cases

- **Rivalry.** Defense, with a minimal field. The dominance state is the reference, the micro-epochs are multiplexed vehicles, and the suppressed face is a target, not a term.
- **Histed's V1 filter; delta-rule layers; Mamba-3.** Assimilation, or dynamics shaped like regulation without being regulation.
- **Attractor networks over contentful vehicles in a minded system.** They meet N1. They are fields only if they also meet admission and the reach condition, with a constraint projected from the model. Counting fields in a brain becomes a real question, which the disunity essay inherits.
- **The cerebellum.** It is excluded by feature 5, because at runtime it predicts without restoring. The falsifier changes accordingly: runtime restoring dynamics in cerebellar cortex, with no experience (AUD, (f)).
- **Thermostats and plants.** They defend references, but their vehicles lack richness. They are cognition at most.
- **Belief revision.** A defended reference at the grain of truth-bearers. It belongs to mindedness, not experience.
- **Minimal states.** A reference defended over a nearly empty projection. The prediction is that they show restoring dynamics, and their absence would be a falsifier.
- **The monastery.** It is minded to a degree, has no field of its own, and has its experiences in its monks' fields in the weak sense of §6.
- **Transformers and hybrids.** Across tokens there is no field (§3). Hybrids remain open, pending the gate test.

## 9. Tests

1. **Terms and targets in rivalry.** The suppressed content should not constrain the dominant state's other features, and the dominant content should.
2. **Overflow.** Hesse–Tsao report that prefrontal cortex reconstructs face shape poorly (under 30 percent explained variance) where IT does well. If the IT gain asymmetry also covers shape dimensions that prefrontal cortex does not carry, then admission-dependent corrections land on distinctions the access-side area does not read. v2 predicts this; broadcast-for-access accounts do not need it. [inferred; it needs the paper's Methods and a feature-wise analysis]
3. **Feedback and gain.** Disrupting feedback from the candidate holder of the reference should abolish the gain asymmetry while the alternation survives. This separates v2 from a pure routing reading, but not from global workspace theory.
4. **Open-loop lifetimes.** Measure intrinsic vehicle lifetimes under playback, and loop latencies under perturbation, in a preparation that allows both.
5. **The cerebellum.** Look for runtime restoring dynamics in cerebellar cortex.
6. **Gates in hybrids.** Does β damp writes that conflict with established associations, and is the protected state itself revised by the writes it admits?
7. **Minimal states.** Look for restoring dynamics after brief intrusions.
8. **Confidence sets the gain.** Confidence in a belief should predict how readily evidence revises it, as a measure of the ratio between defense gain and revision gain. Manipulating confidence alone should change the revision rate.
9. **Quarantine.** Entertaining a view should change felt plausibility and affect, but not later inference. Errors found in the entertained view should still transfer to one's own view where the two share a structure.
10. **A token alive at the switch.** Align recordings to perceptual switches and track tokens alive at the moment of the switch. If admission is a relation, their gain flips mid-life. If it is fixed when a token forms, the asymmetry changes only as tokens are replaced. Monkeys do not report switches, so this needs human intracranial recordings or a no-report proxy, such as optokinetic nystagmus.
11. **Coherence or monitoring.** In rivalry, hold content coherence fixed and manipulate only the reliability of one eye's channel. If dominance follows reliability, admission is decided by monitoring, which is higher-order in deciding but not in constituting (§7). If it follows coherence alone, v2 is first-order throughout.

- **Permissiveness inside a brain.** Fields may be plural wherever an attractor network meets admission and the reach condition. The theory then owes a count, and the disunity literature becomes evidence for or against it.
- **Promotion.** An idea that was exploratory is promoted to a foundation, on the strength of the delta-rule, Mamba-3 and Hesse–Tsao cases. Its history carries no weight.

## Verdict addendum, 27 October 2026

Status: archival. The body above is left as it was. There will be no v3; the mindedness essay is the vehicle for what survives. These verdicts come from a conversation about Michał Wójcik's dialogue *Whether There Is a Well-Defined Hard Problem* (reply draft in `notes/research/hard-problems-reply-to-michal.md`). Items marked (user) are the user's own positions. The rest is Claude's analysis, which the user accepted.

**Unity (§5) survives.** "One economy of error over mutually assessable vehicles" is close to what we now call psychological unity: whether something constitutes a single broadcasting economy, with a broadcast that returns (ESOM:57 excludes the one-way channel). "Records suffice for the unity of a mind, but not for a field" also stands.

**Shared defense (Summary, §4) is rejected.** A broadcasting economy does not entail coherence maintenance (user). Broadcast that returns can spread a contradiction as easily as resolve one. Maintenance needs a process that registers conflicts between truth-bearers and revises, and broadcast is at most its precondition. Counterexamples are a blackboard architecture with no consistency checker, a transformer's context, and fragmented belief (Lewis, Egan). The asymmetry is that a mind's unity survives incoherence, while a field does not survive missing binding. So the five conditions split two ways:
- *What makes something a mind*: accuracy-responsiveness as a disposition (MbD:67), not maintained coherence.
- *What makes it one mind*: the economy.

Defense belongs to experience, and through U1 to personhood. The symmetry that remains is that phenomenality and mindedness are both regulation, at vehicle grain and at truth-bearer grain. Their unities are asymmetric: binding achieves phenomenal unity, while psychological unity is a structure of availability.

**Psychological unity underlies phenomenal unity (user).** With the local variant disowned (MON:116), binding into one field needs global regulation. Every field therefore lies within an economy, which bounds it without fixing it. One economy may hold more than one field (user). Nested experiences, multiple fields and overlapping economies (monk and monastery) leave "how many beings?" without a privileged answer. The metaphysics needs no such count. Ethics needs a rule for proportioning moral consideration. The rule we argue for is that a shared experience is not added once per owner (MON:124 already counts occurrences, not ownerships). Do not claim that a whole is not added to its parts. A field is more than the sum of its part-experiences, since its joints are experienced (user). A measure that is additive over disjoint parts would deny this. Keep this ethical measure apart from the reality measure of *Presence and Actuality* ("Suffering and reality measure"). That one weighs occurrences from beneath the grain of physics, like Born weights on branches, and nested experiences share their occurrence's weight. The mereology of minds is insulated from it. Their distribution across rulial space (Boltzmann brains, plenitude) is not.

**The local variant is rejected for phenomenality in general, not only for animals (user).** Tracking, assessment and intervention also belong to minded regulation (ESOM:61), so binding is what makes regulation experiential. The local variant gives that criterion up and cannot say what phenomenality is. The general claim has the status of *Grain*'s identity: necessary if true, known a posteriori, with our own experiences fixing the kind as samples fix "water". Read "local" and "global" by how far a loop reaches, not by anatomy. Localist compositional binding that yields a field relabels non-local binding (user). Composition that does not return yields no field over its parts. Conceiving such a convergent architecture is conceiving the rival theory, not a counterexample.

**The dis-joint Martian.** It is possible, and two fields within one economy explain it without the local variant (user). Dis-jointness that is presented as a relation is a joint, however weak. Between genuine fields, the economy registers the dis-jointness cognitively.

**Binding has dimensions (user).** An imagined apple shares the perspectival format of the seen desk. The represented space carries both, but no experienced space does: the two are joined in time and dis-joint in space. Reality monitoring maintains the dis-joint by exempting imagined vehicles from cross-checks with the scene. Its failures show it at work: the Perky effect, hallucination, and imagery projected onto the scene. Togetherness in content without togetherness in experience is evidence that content does not fix phenomenal binding.

**Joints are phenomenal (user).** Seeing them is like seeing movement rather than a change of position, like the Dalmatian rather than democracy. In the Dalmatian, recognition of the whole changes how the same patches look. That favors corrections that return to the feature vehicles over convergence on a new vehicle. Mooney-image results point the same way (Hsieh, Vul and Kanwisher 2010, cited from memory and not yet checked). The empirical argument needed is narrow: do binding corrections return to feature-level vehicles? Use cross-modal examples; ESOM:65's cup is visual-only, and local recurrence could bind it.

**Working memory** means active maintenance within the thick present, not recall of earlier moments.

**The knowledge-grounding conjecture** (§ Summary, History) is abandoned (user). It held that phenomenal consciousness evolved to provide epistemic assurance. The current view is that experience contributes epistemically when present, through reliability assessment and binding, but that knowledge does not require it. v2's "promotes a sharpened version" should be read in that light.

**Probe cases for the mindedness essay:**
- perceptual imagination;
- absorption, such as a daydream while driving, as the best everyday candidate for two fields in one economy;
- aphantasia, where the vehicles are intact (many aphantasics dream visually) but the voluntary write path is missing; this is a human case of *Grain*'s Mary.
