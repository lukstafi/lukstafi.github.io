# Citation check: logical uncertainty essay outline (1 Nov 2026)

Checked against `logical-uncertainty-essay-outline.md` by three parallel research passes. "Full text" means the checker read the source; "abstract" means bibliographic data or abstract only. Downloads were kept in the session scratchpad, not in the repository.

## Corrections that change the outline

1. **Logical induction does not reweight traders multiplicatively.** TradingFirm combines traders with fixed weights 2^(−k−b)·Budgeter(b, trader k) (eq. 5.3.2); a trader's influence is cut off only when losses exceed its budget. "Wealth as memory, as in Hedge" is an analogy, not the construction.
2. **Logical induction disciplines bets without settlement too.** "our notion of exploitation rewards traders for arbitrage, even if they arbitrage between sentences that never 'pay out'." Discipline comes from consistency with what has been proven, not only from sentences settling.
3. **Convergence rates are uncomputable, not merely absent.** §5.5, Prop. 5.5.1 "Uncomputable Convergence Rates".
4. **Polymarket arbitrage is almost all within single markets.** Of $39,587,585 (markets resolved 1 Apr 2024 – 1 Apr 2025): ≈$10.6M single-condition YES/NO rebalancing, ≈$29.0M multi-condition rebalancing, and only ≈$95K combinatorial arbitrage across logically linked markets (4 of 13 dependent US-election pairs). The bookmaker essay's sentence ("Some came from single markets … and some came from combinations of linked markets") is accurate but the outline's "linked-market arbitrage is logical incoherence priced in dollars" overstated it.
5. **MIRI's direction change.** 21 Dec 2020: the undisclosed 2017 "new research directions" "largely failed"; agent foundations is not named. 4 Jan 2024: "policy and communications will be a higher priority for MIRI than research going forward." June 2024: the Agent Foundations team, including Garrabrant, left MIRI to work independently.
6. **"Odd loop" is not Doyle's term.** Doyle 1979 says "unsatisfiable circularity" (with the p :- not p case: "the TMS must make F in if and only if it makes F out"). "Odd loop" is from Charniak, Riesbeck & McDermott, *Artificial Intelligence Programming* (1980), §16.7. Write "Doyle's unsatisfiable circularity (an 'odd loop')".
7. **Levesque's tractability needs both sides in CNF.** Theorem 3: for propositional KB and α in CNF, classical implication is co-NP-complete, tautological entailment is O(mn).
8. **Urn models still leave propositional omniscience.** Jago 2006: under Rantala's semantics every agent knows all propositional tautologies (Rantala Thm 1). Urn models remove omniscience only for quantificational depth.
9. **Mixed-strategy game values are already compositional on finite models.** Galliani & Mann 2013, lottery semantics. The §4 open point must move to infinite structures and bounded players.
10. **Near-hit on compositional credences from semantic games.** Pallavi Sudhir & Tran-Thanh 2024 (below). Cite and distinguish.
11. **Prover–estimator debate v2 has six authors and fixes v1's main theorem.**
12. **Logical induction does not cite Shafer, Vovk, Foster, Vohra or Dawid.** Do not claim lineage. Kosoy names defensive forecasting a precursor.

## Philosophy and logic

- **Hacking 1967.** "Slightly More Realistic Personal Probability", *Philosophy of Science* 34(4), 311–325, doi:10.1086/288169. Full text. §6 "Personal possibility": "just as logical probability is related to logical possibility, so personal probability demands a concept of personal possibility"; "For me, when is a proposition possible? When I do not know it to be false." Objects of personal probability are sentences.
- **Garber 1983.** "Old Evidence and Logical Omniscience in Bayesian Confirmation Theory", in Earman (ed.), *Testing Scientific Theories*, Minnesota Studies 10, 99–131. Full text. "we can throw such sentences ['h ⊢ e'] into our problem-relative language as atomic sentences, unanalyzed and unanalyzable wholes".
- **I. J. Good.** Dynamic probability: "Dynamic probability, computer chess, and the measurement of knowledge", *Machine Intelligence 8* (1977), 139–150, reprinted in *Good Thinking* (1983). Evolving probability: "Corroboration, explanation, evolving probability, simplicity and a sharpened razor", BJPS 19 (1968), 123–143. Type II rationality: "How rational should a manager be?", *Management Science* 8(4) (1962), 383–393; "Twenty-seven principles of rationality" (1971), ch. 2 of *Good Thinking*. Type II = expected utility net of deliberation cost. Abstracts and secondary sources only.
- **Gaifman 2004.** "Reasoning with limited resources and assigning probabilities to arithmetical statements", *Synthese* 140(1/2), 97–119. Full text. "The restriction on resources is expressed in the framework by a restriction to some finite set of sentences." "The probability function is partial … The probability axioms are supposed to hold, as long as we are within the probability's domain of definition." He avoids bounding proof length because bounded implication is not transitive; credits Hacking as "groundbreaking". **Gaifman 1964**, "Concerning measures in first order calculi", *Israel J. Math.* 2(1), 1–18, doi:10.1007/BF02759729.
- **Seidenfeld, Schervish & Kadane 2012.** "What Kind of Uncertainty Is That? …", *J. Phil.* 109(8/9), **516–533** (not 48–65). Full text. Opens with Savage's remote digit of π. - **Rantala 1975.** "Urn models: a new kind of non-standard model for first-order logic", J. Phil. ## MIRI, logical induction, forecasting, debate

- **Garrabrant, Benson-Tilsen, Critch, Soares & Taylor, "Logical Induction"**, arXiv:1609.03543 (v5 Dec 2020); short version TARK 2017, EPTCS 251:221–235. Full text. Def. 3.0.1: "there is no efficiently computable trader T that exploits P relative to D"; exploitation = plausible net worth "bounded below, but not bounded above". Traders are polynomial-time, "continuous piecewise-rational functions of the market prices". MarketMaker uses "an approximate fixed point (guaranteed to exist by Brouwer's fixed point theorem)"; continuity required "for precisely this purpose". Properties: Limit Coherence (4.1.2), Provability Induction (4.2.1), Learning Pseudorandom Frequencies (4.4.2; Ack(n,n)th digit of π; abstract "≈ 10% probability to 'the nth digit of π is a 7'"), Introspection (4.11.1), Paradox Resistance (4.11.2: χ^p_n ↔ P_n(χ^p_n) < p gives lim P_n = p), Self-Trust (4.12.4). §7.2: "We attribute the success of logical inductors in the face of paradox to the continuity conditions." Related work names Hacking, Garber, Good 1950, Aaronson 2013, Gaifman, Hutter et al., probabilistic numerics, MLNs, Solomonoff; closest market precedent "Beygelzimer, Langford, and Pennock (2012)" (AAMAS 2012, Kelly bettors). Corrections 1–3, 12.
- **Shafer & Vovk 2001**, *Probability and Finance: It's Only a Game!*, Wiley; successor *Game-Theoretic Foundations for Probability and Finance* (2019).
- **Vovk, Takemura & Shafer 2005**, "Defensive forecasting", AISTATS 2005, 365–372, arXiv:cs/0505083. Full text. Thm 1: "for any continuous strategy for Skeptic there exists a strategy for Forecaster that does not allow Skeptic's capital to grow". Proof picks a zero of a continuous function ("choose p_n so that S_n(p_n) = 0"), the one-dimensional form of the fixed-point move. On the liar-like case: "since all test functions I are continuous and so cannot sharply distinguish between the cases p_n < 0.5 and p_n ≥ 0.5, we do not have any contradiction." Mixing Skeptic strategies is "fundamental", parallel to budgets.

## Novelty searches

- **"Probability relative to a class of tests" across fields.** Not stated as one principle, but close: Barak, "A different type of pseudo" (Windows on Theory, 1 Oct 2015), opening "The probability that the [n]-th digit of [π] is 7 is 10%", pseudorandomness as "a computational analog of frequentist probability" and SoS pseudo-distributions as "a computational analog of Bayesian probability", proposed for number-theoretic heuristics; companion "Bayesianism, frequentism, and the planted clique" (13 Apr 2016). ARC, Christiano, Hilton, Lincoln, Neyman & Xu, "Towards a Law of Iterated Expectations for Heuristic Estimators", arXiv:2410.01290 (2024): multiaccuracy for estimating mathematical expressions, citing logical induction and Barak. Kosoy & Appel, "Optimal Polynomial-Time Estimators", arXiv:1608.04112 (2016). No source joins outcome indistinguishability with logical induction.
- **Logical induction vs defensive forecasting/multicalibration.** Kosoy, "Forecasting using incomplete models", arXiv:1705.04630, appendix "Dominant Forecasters": "Similar ideas were investigated … under the name 'defensive forecasting'". Kosoy, comment of 4 Jun 2021 on Xu's "An Intuitive Guide to Garrabrant Induction": defensive forecasting is "a historical precursor of Garrabrant induction". Perdomo & Recht, "In Defense of Defensive Forecasting", arXiv:2506.11848 (2025), and Noarov & Roth, "Defensive Boosting", arXiv:2608.13554 (2026), tie defensive forecasting to OI and multicalibration, without logical induction. The three-way comparison appears new.
- **Presumption of independence as a nonmonotonic logic.** Prior art: Grosof, "Non-monotonicity in probabilistic reasoning", UAI 1986 (arXiv:1304.3087), "Maximization of Conditional Independence" as a default, via circumscription; Goldszmidt, Morris & Pearl 1993 (max-ent as nonmonotonic logic); Bacchus, Grove, Halpern & Koller, random worlds, "default assumptions of independence"; Halpern & Koller on representation dependence; Benferhat, Dubois & Prade, ECAI 1994. Batens's adaptive logics of inductive generalization presume uniformity, the nearest adaptive analogue. An adaptive logic with dependences as abnormalities, and the CLuN/MLN glut bridge, appear unclaimed.
- **Compositional credences from semantic games between bounded players.** Near-hit: Pallavi Sudhir & Tran-Thanh, "Betting on what is neither verifiable nor falsifiable", arXiv:2402.14021 (2024): polynomial-time agents trade on a modified Hintikka Verifier–Falsifier game over arithmetic; prices converge to 1 when Verifier has a computable winning strategy; explicitly an alternative to Garrabrant induction for pricing sentences atomically; a compositional measure is future work. Also Boyer & Sandu, "Between proof and truth", *Synthese* 187(3) (2012), 821–832; time-bounded GTS for ATL/μ-calculus (Goranko et al.). Credences defined compositionally by game value between complexity-bounded players on infinite structures still look open.
- **Self-distillation as logical uncertainty.** Nothing ties it to logical induction or settlement. Nearby: Lombrozo, "Learning by thinking in natural and artificial minds", TiCS 28(11) (2024), 1011–1022; Huang, Block, Foster et al., "Self-Improvement in Language Models: The Sharpening Mechanism", arXiv:2412.01951; arXiv:2601.05280 (2026) on closed-loop distribution matching in Solomonoff terms.
