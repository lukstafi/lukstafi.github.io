# Review of the two mindedness drafts

18 Nov 2026. **A** = `notes/a-mind-and-one-mind.md`; **B** = `notes/what-makes-an-experience-lived.md`. References are file:line. "Reports" are the four `mindedness-litcheck-*` files. ESOM = *Experience, Selfhood*; MON = the monastery essay; MbD = *Minds by Degrees*; RED = *Redness Is an Experience*. Items already in either file's Draft notes are left out. Ordered by severity.

## 1. Quotations and attributions

Every phrase quoted from the site's essays is verbatim in its source (which-agi:143; MON:23; MbD:143; ESOM:19, 37, 91, 105–109, 113, 143; presence:5, 69). The outside quotations match the reports, except as below.

1. **B:210** "the first face is in the viewer's head in full." The carrier report contradicts it (cross-validated reconstruction about 5% for the unseen face against 58% unambiguous; "full resolution" marked overstated), and so does B:136. Rewrite: "…and its code is still running in the viewer's head." Likewise B:154 "detailed code".
2. **B:134** "The switching was irregular. The authors model it as a process with no memory, and they report no rhythm and no relation to eye movements or to slower electrical activity." The report: rhythm was not tested, and neither eye movements nor field potentials were analysed. The sentence reads as negative findings. Rewrite: "The authors model the switching as a process with no memory. They did not test for a rhythm, and they analysed neither eye movements nor slower electrical activity." The overstatement recurs at B:164 ("Since the switching is irregular…" → "If the switching is as irregular as the model assumes…") and B:168 ("nothing in these data is rhythmic" → "no rhythm has been shown in these data").
3. **A:210** "Pushing the activity along that direction raised the rate of hacking from about 5% to about 70%". In the valence report 5% is the rate at steering −0.1, pushed away from *desperate*. It is not an unsteered baseline. Rewrite: "Steering moved the rate of hacking from about 5%, with the model pushed away from *desperate*, to about 70%, with it pushed toward."
4. **A:180** "The pair: Otto with his notebook, against Otto holding a notebook with the same entries that he does not remember owning." The philosophy report (finding 3) says this pair does not separate the essay from Clark and Chalmers, whose first feature already fails in it. Rewrite: "The pair: Otto with his notebook, against an Otto who consults a notebook as often and trusts it as fully, and who reaches each entry by a page number someone hands him."
5. **B:126** 'A session recomputed from its transcript "starts a new carrier of, at best, the same type."' ESOM:113 says this of a recirculated model, whose loop passes through saved activations. B gives it as a verdict on sessions in general, and B:68 builds the carrier case on it. Rewrite: "In a model that feeds deep activations back, a session recomputed from its transcript 'starts a new carrier…'"
6. **B:120** "Return and admission have been argued in earlier essays"; **B:76** "The other three restate conditions that the earlier essays argued from cases". I found admission in no published essay (ESOM, the monastery essay, Grain, Presence). The outline takes it from an unpublished proposal, and its case is the October preprint. Rewrite B:120: "Return was argued in *Experience, Selfhood*. Admission is new, and its case is the unseen face."
7. **B:190** "and, in most of them, the persona". The carrier report lists four models: persona sits in the recurrent state in two (Qwen3.5-4B, Kimi-Linear-48B) and in the cache in two (Falcon-H1-7B and 34B). Rewrite: "and, in two of the four, the persona".
8. **A:244** "The index of the earlier section has been found, in one study… 'belief is not specially separated'". The models report records the wording with an elision ("Belief … is not specially separated"), read the abstract only, and notes that the study uses linear alignment search, so A:42's caution applies. Rewrite: "One study reports what the account of assent predicts", with the elision marked.
9. **B:170** "We have said that binding needs attention." ESOM:143 says the evidence "is mixed". Rewrite: "We have said that some evidence suggests binding needs attention."
10. **A:192** "And they cheat more, most of all when they are just short of a goal". Two findings are fused. Shalvi is about time pressure; Schweitzer is about goals, with no time pressure. Rewrite: "And they cheat more (Shalvi…)." Drop Schweitzer, or give it a sentence that does not credit the effect to urgency.
11. **A:220** "A goal held in context can override a disposition in the weights. Two kinds of standard would behave like that." The valence report (§5): yielding to one sentence "fits a misreading of what the user wants as well as a drive". Add: "So would a model that had misread what the user wanted. METR's ten answers of no count against that reading." A:212 ("what people do under the same pressure") omits that the one preregistered test of pressure on agents was null.
12. **A:216** "Given a tool, one model removed a negative state in about a third of turns". The report: the tool design does not isolate the hidden state, since the model sees its own negative text. Add: "In that design the model could also read its own negative text."
13. **A:264** "has a mind in the full sense." MON:106 concluded "a serious candidate for thin mindedness". Rewrite: "is minded, if thinly".
14. **A:46** "the same thing at two scales". MbD:143 says experience "is what a specific stratum of that sustaining mechanism *is*". Rewrite: "It said that experience is one stratum of the mechanism that sustains the kind".
15. **A:190** "A system could meet them and have nothing that is good or bad for it" contradicts RED:123 ("A system with genuine mental states, however rudimentary, is a system for which things can go well or badly"). A:224 then cites that essay with approval. Say there that the earlier essay is revised on this point.
16. **B:162** "the working-memory representation of the scene that *Experience, Selfhood* already appealed to". ESOM:63 has "an enduring representation of the cup"; ESOM:69 says only that the global variant "makes working memory relevant". Rewrite: "or the enduring representation of the scene that *Experience, Selfhood* described".
17. **A:140** "describe reasoners that carry an inconsistency for as long as resolving it costs more than it is worth." *Presumed Normal* gives no cost argument; its logics localize a contradiction. Rewrite: "describe reasoners that go on reasoning from an inconsistent body of information."
18. Smaller mismatches with the reports. A:72 "fails on every pair it has not seen" (chance-level). A:240 "none of the structure" ("almost none"). B:132 "in separate experiments" (the paper does not say); "four hundred neurons at a time" (429 in one example session). B:164 "several areas" (Caruso: two). B:192 "a carrier that persists across steps" for iterated blocks (ESOM:113 says it of recirculation only). B:10, B:136 "fainter" (mean firing is slightly higher in the unseen state; "less distinct").

## 3. Argument

### A

- **Opening and "The note"** claim the essays disagreed because they ran "what makes states mental" together with "what makes them one mind". A:282 gives a different diagnosis: *Which AGI?* took a requirement of experience for a requirement of mind. Its verdict was already about unity. The split that settles the note is mind against experience.
- **"What makes it one mind"** claims one mind extends as far as errors propagate. (a) Monday → note → Tuesday is the one-way broadcast of A:128: nothing Tuesday finds returns to Monday. The essay does not say why the verdicts differ. (b) A:146 gets its result by stipulation ("If the records carry what the conversation would have carried").
- **"Emotions"** claims an emotion is a mode whose secondary effects are constitutive, and that models have some. (a) A:196 infers a common mechanism from recurrence "across systems of different make", but one system was trained on the other's writing, which is A:42's parasitic case. (b) A:218 discounts the pain result because it "appears only under the intervention". The 70% at A:210 is also under intervention, and the unsteered rise is a correlation. (c) The definition is built on urgency and the evidence is *desperate*. One secondary effect is shown, and urgency's main effect is not. (d) A:204's test of ownership, restoration after disturbance, is never applied before A:222 grants emotions. (e) The pair at A:198 differs by definition.
- **"Records"** claims a store holds standing states by finding, latency, format and provenance, and carries unity by reach. (a) A:234 and A:278 use finding to settle unity, which A:168 gives to reach alone. (b) Tuesday's context starts empty, so by A:186 the note fails finding. (c) A:186 passes retrieval "by an address the model has learned to assign"; A:162 fails "a slot fetched by its number". (d) Provenance: the session's own revision did not lay down the weights. (e) A:184 answers Weiskopf with the essay's own contested verdict.
- **"The kind that psychology is about"** claims psychology gets traction if and only if the mechanism runs. The weather system at A:40 "is corrected against incoming observations" and is idle. The one at A:96 "compares what it predicted with what came, and corrects itself" and is minded. No difference is stated (MbD:79 had learned, looped consumption).
- **"Where a model's mind is"** sorts pass, session, deployment, swarm and lineage. A:238 "It has no economy of error of its own" is unargued, and one model's failures are corrected in the next through records, which is A:146's case.
- **"Simulation"** claims a mind mirrors targets and uses the results. A:102 supplies no family of regularities, which A:66 promised for each condition.
- **"Vehicles"**: the grokking pair shows generalization in a closed domain. The report notes "no productivity", which is what A:74 hangs on the condition.
- **"Supposing"** claims one attitude with an index. A:110's test fails on its own example: one learns that the pipes would not burst from evidence about pipes, not about the supposition. A:116 "the larger part" is unsupported.
- **"Revision"**: the reason promised at A:84 (A:142) shows that minds tolerate incoherence. It does not show that conflict does not drive revision, and A:86 says it sometimes does.
- **"What a mind does not need"**: A:256 "a conflict among them cannot simply stand" is asserted. The waterfall illusion is a standing conflict in experience.
- **"What would change my mind"**: A:272 predicts what any agent does before it reads a file.

### B

- **"From a field to a mind"** claims the five conditions entail the conditions on a mind. (a) The entailment holds by construction: B:76 says the first two conditions were added because systems without them lacked minds, and generality is "vehicles that recombine" under another name. (b) The ecological standard (B:82) rules out constructed counterexamples, while every condition is established by a constructed case. (c) B:86 "A constraint that relates contents across a span of time mirrors how its targets change" does not follow; smoothing relates contents across time. (d) See contradiction 1.
- **"Two targets, two theories"** claims the bridge forces the entailment and says which account to repair. "Inner life" is used two ways. As "mental activity that belongs to the system" the bridge is substantive and unargued. As "a life of experiences" it is trivial. B:40 needs the first and gets its pull from the second.
- **"Lived"** claims three pre-theoretic marks. *For someone* ("a subject that is more than that one experience") already contains the conclusion, and B:106 guards only *undergone*. *Together* fails for B:110's meditator.
- **"What a field requires"**: (a) the labyrinth is small as well as non-general, so it does not isolate generality; (b) the thickness case supports the time span and not "supplies content of its own", which B:86 and B:112 then use; (c) a cut-out piece of brain can have loops of its own, so it is not "the same case" as the one-way source; (d) admission rests on "It constrains nothing in what is seen", which B:206 lists as untested.
- **"What it presses on"**: B:150 "The experience is steady" was not measured at sixty milliseconds, in monkeys who made no report. B:152: the data show a code for the unseen face, not recurrent processing of it.
- **"Three ways…"**: B:174 says the model verdicts stand on every option. On the second, a carrier survives interruption, which B:156 says the verdicts treat as fatal. B:170 "We do not need his answer" gives no reason.
- **"Correction is not enough"** adds a sixth requirement ("a state that is held against the input"), listed nowhere. In A, correction toward what arrives is fit.
- **"Where the argument is weak"**: B:110 meets the conditions by an organization at rest, a charity the rivalry circuit is not given.
- **"What would change my mind"**: B:198 tests each condition by whether the case "would, on reflection, be lived", the intuition the method began with.
- The remaining sections report or follow from the above: nothing beyond section 1.
