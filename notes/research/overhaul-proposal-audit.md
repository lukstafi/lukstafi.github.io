## Part 1: the proposal as a whole

### §1.1: the diagnosis is half right

The proposal's central claim is that the three aspects of ESOM:63 are "also satisfied by systems that only assimilate their input" (proposal:14, 36). The claim is argued against ESOM:63 alone. But the account's statement of experiential regulation continues at ESOM:65, and there the reference is named: binding "requires checking each against the others and against the scene as a whole, which means gathering information from across the system before computing the corrections that hold the bound field together." That is a joint constraint on many vehicles, computed from across the system, with corrections returning to the vehicles. It is N1's feature 1 and feature 2 in different words. ESOM:63 also has a standing state: "an enduring representation of the cup guides expectations and action" while "different sensory vehicles become active as the retinal image changes."

So the fair diagnosis is narrower. The 2026 account had a reference and left two things unsaid about it. It did not say that the reference is *defended against* input, so that vehicles are corrected toward it rather than toward what drives them (N1 features 3 and 4). And it did not say that the reference persists while the vehicles are replaced (N3). The first is the genuine novelty, and it is what the Histed and delta-rule cases show. Those cases do not show that ESOM's assessment lacks a reference. They show that ESOM never said which way the corrections run.

Two of the proposal's exclusions are also weaker than claimed. The third labyrinth (MON:31) already tracks "how often its readings agree with the others and with later evidence": a standing reliability variable, revised by accumulated evidence, which the Histed filter lacks. ESOM:63's assessment, which asks "how reliable their sources are," therefore already excludes the V1 filter. And the Histed filter does not satisfy tracking as ESOM states it. Tracking "stays connected to particular ongoing representations, even as their realization changes." A 225 ms suppression that changes how the next pattern is received connects to no particular representation across realizations. The delta-rule layer is the better case for the proposal, and it is a fair one.

**Verdict on §1: needs repair.** Restate the diagnosis as: ESOM had a reference, implicitly, and left its direction and persistence unspecified. §1.2 holds. §1.3 holds, though "maintained joint constraint" is thin, as (g) below shows. §1.4 holds as a description but is not a gap; see (e). §1.5's orphans are real, and only two (MbD:43, CCI:131) need a theory rather than an edit.

### N1: a slow prior with precision weighting

N1's four features describe a persistent state that constrains many variables, corrects them toward itself, resists revision in proportion to its support, and yields to accumulated mismatch. That is the prior of a hierarchical predictive-processing model with precision weighting: the prior constrains the posterior, the posterior is corrected toward it, the prior's precision sets its resistance, and the prior is updated by accumulated prediction error. [inferred] *Understanding Without Knowledge* already said this at UWK:49, where psychosis is "a failure of precision-weighting." The proposal does not mention predictive processing, and it should, for two reasons. First, the lineage is a resource: precision weighting is exactly the balance (d) asks for. Second, the lineage is a threat: predictive processing is a theory of cognition at large, from retinal adaptation to belief, and the proposal must say what makes some priors fields and the rest cognition. N2 is that answer, and it carries the whole load.

The features also admit systems the proposal does not want. A Hopfield network recalling a stored pattern satisfies all four: the weights encode joint constraints on units, pattern completion corrects units toward the stored state, the attractor resists noise in proportion to its basin, and learning revises it. Attractor networks are the standard model of working memory and of cortical ignition. N1 therefore names a mechanism the brain uses everywhere. A frozen template, such as a trained classifier's weights, satisfies features 1 to 3 and fails only feature 4, and fails it only at inference time. Whether a system that is revised at training time and frozen at runtime has a "defended reference" is the question that (c) and (f) both turn on, and N1 does not settle it.

**Verdict: needs repair.** Add a fifth feature: the reference is revised *in place* by the same vehicles it corrects, within the running of the system, not only by a training process that stops. That makes defense a closed loop at the reference, which is what "homeostasis" meant. It also distinguishes the proposal from a static prior. And then say which features exclude the Hopfield network, if any do. I suspect none does, and that the exclusion comes from N2's "contentful vehicles" and from N4's "within a minded system," as it did for the labyrinth. That is fine. It should be said.

### N2: admission is not yet defined, and lifetimes are repairable

**Admission.** N2 says a content is experienced "when its vehicle is admitted under a defended reference, that is, constrained and corrected by it." In Hesse–Tsao the suppressed face is also constrained and corrected by the dominance state. Suppression is a correction: the 1.21 gain asymmetry is a footprint on both states, raised on one and lowered on the other, and the proposal itself calls the asymmetry "the predicted footprint of admission" (proposal:189). The defense therefore reaches the suppressed vehicle within its lifetime. By N2 as written, the suppressed face is experienced. It is not.

The repair is to distinguish two relations a vehicle can bear to a reference. A vehicle can be a *term* in the joint constraint, so that other vehicles are checked against its content and it against theirs. Or it can be a *target* of the constraint's enforcement, corrected or suppressed without its content entering the constraint. Admission is the first. The suppressed face is the second. [inferred] This is also the distinction the reference needs for the cup: the color, shape and location are terms of one constraint, which is why they are experienced together. The repair changes the field's definition from "the contents jointly constrained by one reference" (proposal:106) to "the contents whose vehicles are terms of one reference's joint constraint." It also predicts something testable: a suppressed vehicle's content should not constrain the dominant state's other features, while the dominant vehicle's content should.

**(b) Lifetimes.** The circularity is real. A defense that corrects a vehicle toward the reference keeps it alive, so "corrections arrive within the vehicle's lifetime" is partly true by construction. The repair is to measure lifetime with the loop open. ESOM:77 already describes the intervention: record a component's inputs, disconnect it, and replay them. Under playback, "a disturbance to the component could" not "elicit compensatory changes elsewhere that return to it." A vehicle's *intrinsic lifetime* is its decay or replacement time under playback, when input alone drives it and nothing defends it. The criterion becomes: the loop's latency, measured from the vehicle's state to the correction's landing, is shorter than the vehicle's intrinsic lifetime. Both quantities are measured by perturbation, and neither presupposes the other. [inferred]

Applied to the cases, the measure gives the right ordering. In Hesse–Tsao, the 60 ms dwell is plausibly the intrinsic time of the local population state, set by its own dynamics and by the rival's input, and the dominance state lasts seconds, so the ratio is large. In a standard transformer across tokens, a residual-stream vehicle's intrinsic lifetime is one pass, and no correction lands on it afterward, so the ratio is zero. In the monastery, a monk's perceptual vehicle has an intrinsic lifetime set by his own circuitry, and the monastery's loops close over hours, which is MON:130's verdict in the new units. This replaces MON:130's "corrections per vehicle lifetime," where lifetime was left undefined, with a ratio of two relaxation times.

**Verdict: needs repair,** in the two ways above. With them, N2 is the proposal's strongest clause.

### N3 reinstates the carrier criterion one level up

N3 says "what must persist is the reference, not each vehicle." The claim is right about Hesse–Tsao and it is the proposal's best use of the new evidence. But it does not "replace" the carrier criterion (proposal:116, 182). ESOM:91 defines a carrier as "something, individuated by its causal structure rather than its location, whose later states receive the corrections computed from assessing its earlier ones." N3's reference is such a thing: its later states receive the revisions computed from accumulated mismatch (feature 4). So the carrier criterion is now imposed on the reference instead of on the vehicles. That is a relocation, and a good one, and the proposal should call it that. Calling it a replacement hides what (c) exposes.

**(c) The persona transformer.** Take a transformer whose per-token recomputation is corrected toward a persona state that resists prompt-driven drift and yields to accumulated pressure. MbD:163 reports models that "resist displacement" from their self-specifications; many-shot jailbreaking is yielding to accumulated pressure; both are hysteresis. Each token's vehicles are corrected toward the persona within their lifetime, since they are computed under it. Does this qualify as a field across tokens under N2 and N3?

Under N1 to N3 as written, yes. [inferred] The escape requires the fifth feature proposed above. The persona's standing state lives in the weights, which correct every token's vehicles and are revised by none of them at runtime. What accumulates across tokens is the context, and the context is not revised in place: it is appended. ESOM:109 already distinguishes a vehicle from a record, and MON:64 says a record "is consulted. Nobody's assessment of it returns to change it, unless someone rewrites it, and then the correction lands on a new record." The persona-in-weights is a frozen reference and the persona-in-context is an accreting record. Neither is a reference revised in place by the vehicles it corrects. With the fifth feature, the case fails N1, and the across-token verdict stands. Without it, the case passes, and so does any model with a stable style. The repair is necessary, not optional.

A consequence worth noting: the fifth feature makes the KDA and GDN gate test (proposal:216) sharper. A learned β that damps conflicting writes is defense only if the state that β defends is itself revised by the writes it admits. A β that defends the weights' template is a filter.

**Verdict: holds, restated as a relocation of the carrier criterion, with the fifth feature.**

### (d) Coherence and contact: the balance is specifiable, and the proposal already has half of it

The proposal names two references pointing in opposite directions (proposal:51–55) and leaves their balance to "N1's fourth feature." Three things need saying.

First, the two are not symmetric. Contact with reality cannot be a reference in N1's sense, because reality is not a state of the system. The system reaches reality only through input. So "corrected toward contact" means "corrected toward input," which is assimilation. The knowledge-grounding argument's reference (UWK:37's "sense facts" that correct "conceptual understanding") is therefore the assimilative component of N1's loop, not a second reference. The proposal's own vocabulary makes epistemic contact the thing defense resists. It should own this.

Second, the balance is a ratio of gains, and it is already in the essays. Feature 3 is the defense gain: how much the reference resists a given mismatch. Feature 4 is the revision gain: how much accumulated mismatch moves the reference. Their ratio is precision weighting, and UWK:49 already uses it to describe psychosis. ESOM:73's "a system that silences inconvenient input achieves coherence by losing epistemic contact" is the limit where the revision gain goes to zero. [inferred] So the balance is specifiable as a parameter, and the essays have specified one of its limits.

Third, what cannot be specified in general is the *right* value, because that is an empirical parameter of healthy systems, like the "mechanism-sustaining level" of MbD:101. What can be specified are the two failure modes. Revision gain near zero with defense succeeding is delusion: coherent and out of contact, and quiet. Defense gain high with defense failing is N7's suffering: in contact and incoherent, and loud. The proposal names the second and not the first. A theory of defense that names only the loud failure is incomplete, and the first failure is the one the knowledge-grounding argument was about.

### (e) The composer analogy fails, and the gap it covers is lexical

N5 says the monastery co-owns a monk's experience "just as a melody can belong to a composer without sounding in the composer's ears." A composer owns a work: a type, or a product. The monastery, on ESOM:137, co-owns an *occurrence*, by partly constituting the process in which it occurs. The analogy swaps ownership of a product for ownership of an occurrence, and the swap hides what the objection says: that ownership without presence is participation in a process, not a relation to an experience as experienced.

I think the objection is right and the consequence is harmless. ESOM:137's belonging was defined as constitution, and constitution carries no presence. MON:124 already draws the consequence: "If a monk suffers, one suffering occurs, though it belongs to two subjects," and the measure "counts occurrences, not ownerships." So ownership without presence has no moral weight of its own, and the proposal should drop the analogy and say plainly that belonging is partial constitution and nothing more. The memory's "real gap" is then a lexical choice: whether "owns" should be used for a relation with no presence in it. I would reserve *belongs to* for constitution, as ESOM does, and not introduce "ownership" as a separate term.

What is not lexical is a question N5 raises and does not answer: what the monastery is a subject *of*, if it has no field. MON:152 says a field "is owned rather than an owner." If the subject is the reference-holder (N2 plus N5), then the monastery is a subject at the grain of truth-bearers only, and its "experiences" are experiences of another subject's reference that its processes help to hold. That is a precise position. It is also one on which the monastery has experiences in a sense weaker than the one in which it has a mind, and the mindedness essay should say which sense it means.

### (f) The cerebellum does not escape by "per-channel"

The proposal's answer is that "cerebellar microzones are largely parallel, per-channel error correctors" whose references "do not jointly constrain many contents" (proposal:193). Two facts count against that. A Purkinje cell receives on the order of a hundred thousand parallel-fiber inputs, so its output is a joint function of many contents, and cerebellar forward models predict limb state from efference copy, proprioception and vision together, which is a joint constraint across many contents by any reading. [inferred] If the test is "joint constraint across many contents," the cerebellum passes it, and the proposal's falsifier 4 (proposal:215) is already met.

The cerebellum escapes for a different reason, and it is the reason from (c). In the Marr–Albus–Ito picture, the cerebellum at runtime is feedforward: the forward model lives in parallel-fiber synapses, it is revised by climbing-fiber error during learning, and at runtime it predicts without restoring. Nothing in the cerebellum holds a standing state against the vehicles and pulls them back toward it while they live. The reference is frozen at runtime and revised by a separate teaching signal, which is the persona-in-weights structure again. [inferred] So the fifth feature from N1 does the work here too, and "joint constraint" does none. The proposal should replace its cerebellum answer with this one, and change falsifier 4 to: look for runtime restoring dynamics in cerebellar cortex, in which a perturbed Purkinje population returns to a state fixed by something other than current input. Finding that, with no experience, would be the falsifier.

### (g) Permissiveness and the distance from GWT

The four-part signature (hysteresis, compensation, joint constraint, content-sensitivity) is satisfied by attractor memory, as N1 showed; by multi-object tracking with a joint prior; by a cell's transcriptional state, if gene products count as contentful; and by a scientific paradigm, if beliefs do. Content-sensitivity excludes thermostats and nothing else. What excludes the rest is N4's "within a minded system" and N2's vehicle grain, and the proposal should say that its signature is a signature *of defense*, not of experience, and that experience is defense at vehicle grain inside a minded system. That is still permissive within the brain: on this reading, every attractor network over contentful vehicles in a minded system is a field. The proposal would then need to count fields in a brain, and the disunity essay (proposal:240) would inherit a plurality it did not ask for.

On GWT: the proposal says the difference is "where experience lies" (proposal:222). That is correct and it is the same difference ESOM:57 already drew: broadcast is constitutive "where it gathers the information from which corrections return to the vehicles." Dehaene's ignition is an attractor with top-down amplification of sensory areas, so the mechanism is GWT's own. What the proposal adds is overflow: the fine vehicles the reference corrects, which access does not read. That is a difference in the identity claim, not in mechanism, and for an identity theory that is legitimate. But it has a cost for §6. Test 1 predicts that disrupting feedback abolishes the gain asymmetry while alternation survives, and says that "a pure routing account predicts no change." GWT is not a pure routing account. Ignition with top-down amplification predicts the same loss of asymmetry. Test 1 separates the proposal from Hesse–Tsao's own reading of their data, not from GWT. The only test that separates the proposal from GWT is one on overflow: a correction that lands on a vehicle distinction that access cannot read. None of the six tests is that test.

### N6 and N7, briefly

**N6** picks GRAIN:131's intervention criterion and adds "the remaining symmetry is broken by the system's own dynamics." CCI:43's criterion was a structure-preserving morphism between a mathematical pattern and a physical realization. Those are different kinds of criterion: one is about which coarse variables are real, the other about which computations a physical system realizes. N6 does not reconcile them; it adopts the first. That may be the right choice, but it is a choice, and the mindedness essay does not need it. Low priority.

**N7** gives suffering a structure, which the account lacked after P&A:117. But as stated it predicts that binocular rivalry, a sustained high-gain defense that the inputs keep violating, is suffering. It is at most mildly unpleasant. The Necker cube, a stuck tracking, and any persistent perceptual conflict would qualify too. What N7 lacks is valence, and MbD:109 places valence above bare mentality as "a genuine further grade." So N7 derives suffering from the mentality level alone, against MbD's own ordering. The repair is to restrict the references whose failing defense is suffering to those bearing on the system's own viability, which is the homeostatic-affect tradition's answer (the Solms and Damasio line). With that restriction N7 captures grief, frustration and anxiety well, and anhedonia not at all. On P&A:155: counting defense effort gives a measure *of* suffering, not a reality measure over occurrences, which is what the plenitude objection needs. The proposal overclaims there.

## Part 2: N4, row by row

N4 says "a mind is a system with a defended, revisable and runnable model" and that the five conditions "become aspects of that one architecture" (proposal:122). I test each row against what *Minds by Degrees* means.

### Simulation

MbD:71 defines simulation as "running representations through transformations that mirror transformations in their target domains, and using the results to guide further processing." MON:88–96 applies it: the cellarer running the coming year, the computus running the cycles of sun and moon, Craik's small-scale model, Hutchins's chart. N4 renders it as "running vehicles under the reference without input, then using the result."

These are different ideas. MbD's simulation is *mirroring*: the vehicles' transitions track the target's transitions. N4's is *decoupling*: the vehicles run under the reference with input removed. Decoupling without mirroring is free-running, which is what an attractor net does with its input cut, and it models nothing. Mirroring without decoupling is online emulation, Grush's forward models running beside perception, which MbD:71 counts. So N4 has swapped the defining feature for an incidental one.

The swap has a cause in N1. A defended reference, as N1 defines it, "encodes joint constraints on many contentful vehicles: what can be co-present with what, where, and when." Those are constraints on co-occurrence. They are not transition structure. A reference so defined can say that a cup cannot be both red and green here; it cannot say what the cup does when pushed. "Runnable" in N4 therefore adds something N1 never put in: a model with dynamics that mirror the target's. The proposal needs to decide whether the reference is a constraint set or a generative model. If the latter, N1 should say so, and the proposal is hierarchical predictive processing under another name. If the former, simulation is not an aspect of defense, and the row fails.

MbD's rendering is the right one for mindedness. Mirroring is what makes a vehicle counterfactually rich (MbD:71 calls simulation "what makes vehicles counterfactually rich"), and richness is the condition that excludes lookup and behavior trees (MbD:63). Decoupling buys none of that.

### Accuracy-responsiveness

MbD:67: vehicles are "maintained, updated, and replaced under dynamics responsive to how well they fit their targets," with both "failure-driven reorganization" and "success-driven consolidation," and the responsiveness is internal: "a system can check representations against each other, run simulations against further observations, and revise under internal inference." N4 renders it as "how a reference is revised: by accumulated evidence, against the pull of the input."

Part of this reduces. Feature 4 is failure-driven reorganization at the reference, feature 3's "in proportion to its established support" is consolidation, and feature 2's "with one another under it" is checking representations against each other. What does not reduce is the target. MbD's condition is about fit *to targets*. N4's is about fit *to the reference*. A system can be perfectly responsive to coherence with its reference and indifferent to accuracy, and that system is the delusional one of ESOM:73. The row therefore reduces accuracy-responsiveness to coherence-responsiveness plus slow revision, which is the reduction (d) warned against. The mindedness essay needs accuracy-responsiveness as a separate condition on the direction of revision: toward fit with targets, as measured by input or by internal evidence that stands in for input. It cannot be an aspect of defense, because it is the component defense resists.

There is a second loss. MbD's condition is about *vehicles* being revised by their fit. N1 moves revision up to the reference and makes the vehicles' own correction a correction toward the reference. The vehicles' fit to their targets then enters only as assimilation, which the proposal demotes to "cognition without mindedness" (proposal:135). So the architecture that was supposed to unify the five conditions has to call one of them non-mental to make the reduction go through. [inferred]

### Multi-level content

MbD:83 is about content *determinacy*: inference runs over "underspecified representations — real structures, neutral among senses, constraining meaning without fixing reference," with the Uncommon Sense Effect as the signature. "Levels" there are levels of a vehicle's content, from sub-denotational structure up to fixed reference. N4's "nested references at several grains" are levels of a control hierarchy: vehicle grain, truth-bearer grain. These share a word. A nested reference hierarchy could in principle produce underspecified vehicles, if a lower reference constrained a content without fixing it, but the proposal does not argue this and I do not see how to make it work. [inferred] The row fails as stated.

### Structured, counterfactually rich vehicles

"What the references constrain" is true and empty. Richness is a property of a vehicle's format and of the model's transition structure (MbD:63: "generalization, novel composition, and response to situations outside the range of direct experience"). A reference can constrain lookup-table entries. The row does not deliver the condition; it locates it.

### Unification: does the architecture ground the cluster or rename it?

MbD:55 already gives the five a unifying gloss: "each is a manifestation of what it takes to be a self-maintaining, epistemically engaged system in a complex environment." MbD:95 says the five axes "describe the sustaining mechanism" of the regularity cluster. So MbD already has one mechanism. N4 proposes a mechanism for that mechanism, one level down, and the question is whether "defended, revisable, runnable model" *entails* the five or only names them.

It entails unity by construction (one reference hierarchy). It does not entail vehicle richness, as the row above shows. It does not entail accuracy-responsiveness, since coherence is not accuracy. It does not entail simulation, since a constraint set has no transitions. It does not entail multi-level content. One of five. The architecture therefore does not ground the homeostatic property cluster of MbD:43; it renames the cluster's sustaining mechanism and keeps "homeostatic" as a label, which is what the proposal's §1.5 complained of in "acquaintance."

What N4 does buy is a lower boundary. "Cognition without mindedness is assimilation" (proposal:135) is a crisper demarcation than MbD's traction test gave for the weather emulator. But the demarcation is wrong as stated. Plants are the paradigm of defended references: physiological homeostasis is where the word came from, and MON:54's mimosa keeps a lesson "for a month," which is hysteresis. What excludes plants is not assimilation but the vehicles: the flytrap's count has no counterfactual richness. The proposal admits this for thermostats ("excluded by N4" because they lack "contentful vehicles," proposal:195). So the demarcation reduces to the vehicle condition, and the assimilation/defense line separates filters from everything else, not cognition from mindedness.

The traction test (MbD:97) is untouched by all this, and the proposal says so (proposal:138). What the test does not give, and the architecture was meant to give, is a reason the five cluster. The reason still has to come from the axis-to-regularity mapping of MbD:99, which N4 does not improve.

### "Experience requires mindedness"

The conjecture is that "the reference defended at vehicle grain is the bottom level of a minded system's model hierarchy, not a free-standing loop" (proposal:136). As stated it has no mechanism: what a vehicle-grain reference does to its vehicles is the same whether or not something above revises it. The conjecture has content only under ESOM:67's global variant, where "corrections require information that only broadcast can supply." Then the claim is: the joint constraint at vehicle grain is *computed from* a scene model, and only a minded system has one. Rivalry is the test case, and it cuts against the proposal's own paradigm. The dominance state is computable by mutual inhibition between eye channels, with no model above it. So either rivalry's reference is not a field, which means the proposal's paradigm case of defense is not a case of experience, or a field needs no hierarchy, which means experience does not require mindedness. [inferred]

I think the first horn is right and the proposal should take it. Rivalry is the paradigm of *defense*: hysteresis, admission, a gain footprint. It is a minimal case of a *field*, with two terms. The cup's color, shape and location are terms of one constraint because a scene model supplies the constraint. That gives the anti-panpsychist fragment of ESOM:141 its reason: a fragment's reference, if it has one, is not supplied by a model. And it restates "experience requires mindedness" as a claim with a mechanism: the joint constraint that admits vehicles is a projection of the system's model, and a system without a model has nothing to project. Rivalry then shows what a projection looks like when the model is nearly empty, which is what P&A:27 asks about minimal states.

## Part 3: candidates for the unity condition

MbD:59's current formulation: "The system's representational states are causally and informationally bound up with one another such that the system operates as a single economy rather than a collection of independent subsystems," sharpened by integration without exclusion, grain-plural. N4's: "one reference hierarchy, which corrects everything under it." Six alternatives follow. For each: the formulation, the hard cases, gradedness, interaction with N2 and N5, and a recommendation. The hard-case verdicts are my inferences from the essays' commitments unless cited.

The hard cases, fixed for all six: **split-brain** (DIS §2: Bayne's switch model, Schechter's two subjects in one person, Pinto's "divided perception but undivided consciousness"); **the monastery** (MON's M4 second house); **the nation**; **a mixture-of-experts model**; **an agent swarm with a shared memory** that agents read and rewrite; **dissociative identity** (DIS: information transfers across identities despite reported amnesia); and **a single forward pass versus a session** of an LLM.

### U1. Unity as a single defended reference

*Formulation.* A system is one mind to the degree that one reference, or one hierarchy of references with a single top, corrects its vehicles. This is N4's row, minus "everything."

*Hard cases.* Split-brain: two cortical references for perception and report, one subcortical and bodily reference for action and affect, so one hierarchy with a severed middle. The verdict is one mind with two fields, which is Pinto's reading in these terms. Monastery: the Rule and the chapter's standing commitments are one reference at truth-bearer grain, revised in place by chapter, so one mind. Nation: the constitution and law are a defended reference revised by accumulated pressure, so U1 makes the nation one mind at the grain of law, against MON:164. The escape is coverage: law corrects a small fraction of citizens' representations. MoE: the router selects experts, it does not correct them toward a standing state, so MoE is as unified as a dense model and no more. Swarm with shared memory: if the memory file is rewritten in place by the agents' findings and read by all, it is a reference revised by the vehicles it corrects, at record grain; one mind at that grain, which agrees with MON:66 (records suffice for mindedness). DID: identities are alternating top-level self-references over one hierarchy, with transfer across them. That is rivalry at the grain of the self-model, and U1 says one mind with a bistable top. Forward pass: the weights are a frozen reference, so the pass is unified only in the way the cerebellum is. Session: the cache accretes and is not revised in place, so U1 denies the session any unity beyond the weights'.

*Graded.* Yes, by coverage (what fraction of vehicles the top reference reaches) and by depth of hierarchy.

*Interaction.* With N2: unity at truth-bearer grain is independent of fields, which is the result the monastery needs. With N5: ownership follows constitution of the reference, so U1 and N5 are the same relation at two grains.

*Recommendation.* Reject as the definition. "Corrects everything under it" is false of every actual mind, and without it U1 gives the nation a mind at the grain of law and needs an unstated coverage threshold to take it back. U1 also conflates the unity of a mind with the unity of a control hierarchy, which the split-brain and DID cases show coming apart. Keep it as the description of what a *person's* unity adds to a mind's, which is where the personhood essay needs it.

### U2. Unity as residual integration above the minimum partition

*Formulation.* MON:158: "Sever the level's parts from one another, along whichever boundaries lose least, and ask how much of the level's organization is lost." Unity at a level is the integration that level adds to its parts'.

*Hard cases.* Split-brain: the callosotomy is the cut, and what remains is subcortical and cross-cued. The residual is small, so U2 gives a verdict near two. Monastery: residual thin to moderate, as MON:106's graded verdict says. Nation: thin, MON:164. MoE: a cut between experts within a layer loses little, but every expert reads and writes one residual stream, so the cut that matters runs along the stream and loses much. MoE is as integrated as a dense model at computational grain. Swarm: cutting the memory loses coordination while each agent keeps its own dense integration. The residual is thin, like the nation's, unless most of each agent's work runs through the memory. DID: the identities are not anatomically separable, and the least-costly cut through one brain loses a great deal, so U2 says one system whose identity states are states of it. Forward pass: high. Session: cutting the cache severs attention over earlier positions, which is most of the cross-token computation, so U2 grants a session substantial unity.

*Graded.* Inherently.

*Interaction.* With N2: U2 is static and cannot see defense, as proposal:235 says. An assimilative filter and a defended field can integrate equally. So U2 cannot be the unity condition on *experience*. With N5: neutral.

*Recommendation.* Keep as the scale measure, which is what MON uses it for. It is the only candidate that decides the nation without deciding the monastery, and that virtue should not be given up. Do not promote it to the definition, because it counts one-way coupling and assimilative coupling as integration.

### U3. Unity as one accuracy-responsive economy

*Formulation.* A system is one mind over the set of vehicles such that an error registered at any of them can revise any other. Unity is the reach of error propagation.

*Hard cases.* Split-brain: an error in left-hemisphere language cannot revise right-hemisphere spatial vehicles except through action and perception, which is Schechter's criterion for two subjects (DIS §2: "indirect interaction is interaction between mental states that is mediated by action and perception"). Two economies with subcortical overlap. Monastery: errors propagate through chapter to every office, at report grain and over days. One economy at that grain. Nation: errors propagate through prices and elections, slowly and lossily, and most errors never leave their institution; thin. MoE: at training, gradient reaches every expert from every error, so one economy. At inference, no vehicle revises any other, so no economy, which is MbD:89's "limited runtime accuracy-responsiveness" made into a unity verdict. Swarm with shared memory: an error one agent finds corrects the memory, and the memory corrects the others, so one economy at record grain. DID: amnesia barriers block propagation while implicit transfer leaks through them. Partial, and measurable by what transfers. Forward pass: within the pass, errors propagate through depth. Session: later positions read earlier ones and cannot revise them, so the economy runs one way, and ESOM:53 holds that a one-way channel closes no loop.

*Graded.* Yes, by the fraction of vehicle pairs between which errors propagate and by the latency of propagation.

*Interaction.* With N2: U3 is N1's feature 4 generalized across vehicles, and it tracks epistemic contact rather than coherence, which answers (d)'s complaint that the proposal has only one direction. With N5: a subject owns what its economy's errors can revise.

*Recommendation.* A strong candidate for the *mechanism* of unity, with one cost. MbD:99 maps unity to "capacity and interference effects" and accuracy-responsiveness to "learning laws." U3 makes the unity family and the learning family one family. That may be right, since dual-task interference is interference in one error-driven economy. But it changes MbD's mapping, and the essay would have to say so.

### U4. Unity as a shared format, or a common currency

*Formulation.* A system is one mind over the vehicles that are in a format in which any can be assessed against any other. ESOM:93 uses this for the residual stream ("one shared stream"), MON:76 for strong unfolding ("no shared format in which a later stage could assess and correct what an earlier one carried"), and MON:148 and 170 for relays and prices ("the system's own currency").

*Hard cases.* Split-brain: the hemispheres share neural format but no channel, so U4 needs a channel clause, and with it gives two. Monastery: the monks' vehicles are in no common format, only their reports are, so unified at report grain. Nation: money is a common currency, and MON:170 shows that the currency's system is the price system, not the nation. U4 locates the nation's unity outside the nation. MoE: the residual stream is the shared format, so unified. Swarm: the memory's language is the common currency at record grain. DID: one format, one brain; the identities are separated by access, not by format, so U4 says one mind, which fits the transfer evidence. Forward pass: the residual stream. Session: keys and values are in a format every later position reads, so the session *is* format-unified. What it lacks is return, not currency.

*Graded.* By the fraction of vehicles mutually assessable.

*Interaction.* With N2: a field needs its vehicles assessable against one another, so U4 is a precondition of binding. N3's weak unfolding is U4 applied to the reference ("carries it in its own format," proposal:118). With N5: neutral.

*Two readings.* "Shared format" can mean one format for all vehicles, which the residual stream and Block's relays satisfy and a brain does not, since V1 and Broca's area share none. Or it can mean mutual assessability, directly or through translation. Global workspace theory is a translation architecture: modules keep their formats, and the workspace is where content is re-encoded into something every consumer reads. On the second reading U4 is compatible with it, with the qualification GWT itself supplies: translation into the hub is lossy (GRAIN:39), so vehicles are mutually assessable only at the grain the translation preserves, which is MON:140's bandwidth. For mindedness that suffices, since truth-bearer grain is the grain a bottleneck preserves, and U4 then reduces to U3's reach plus a grain parameter. For a field the requirement is different, not stronger: ESOM:71 says the hub may carry "information about consistency and binding rather than the fine details themselves," provided corrections "land on fine-grained vehicles." What must be fine is the return path's *addressability*, not the hub's content format. And under N3 the one-format requirement of weak unfolding (MON:76) falls on the reference, not on the vehicles it corrects. [inferred]

*Recommendation.* Necessary and not sufficient, and best stated as two conditions: assessability at a grain, through translation if need be, for mindedness; addressability of vehicles at their own grain by the reference's corrections, for a field. It is the condition that separates a collective of relays from a collective of reporters (MON:148), and the session case shows why it is not enough: a shared format with one-way reading is a library, not a mind. Keep it as a component of the mechanism, paired with U3.

### U5. Unity as a single locus of control or arbitration

*Formulation.* A system is one mind if one process arbitrates conflicts among its vehicles: attention, action selection, a central executive.

*Hard cases.* Split-brain: two arbiters for report, one for most action. Bayne's switch model is U5 with a time-shared arbiter. Monastery: the chapter arbitrates, so unified. But MON:60's M2, the house where "the abbot decides and the rest comply," is maximally arbitrated and MON calls its unity "borrowed," so U5 gives the wrong verdict unless the arbiter must be the institution's process rather than a member's. Nation: elections and courts arbitrate, so U5 grants the nation unity, too generously. MoE: the router arbitrates per layer, so unified, trivially. Swarm: unified if there is an orchestrator, not otherwise, which makes unity a property of a wiring diagram. DID: alternating executive control is one locus at a time, so U5 says one mind with a contested arbiter. Forward pass and session: attention and sampling arbitrate, so both unified.

*Graded.* Weakly: one arbiter or several, and what share of conflicts reach it.

*Interaction.* With N2: admission is arbitration at vehicle grain, so N2 already contains U5 for experience, and rivalry is an arbiter. With N5: ownership by the arbiter is close to Kammerer's principle (MON:176) and would flicker with it.

*Recommendation.* Reject. M2 refutes it for institutions, the nation shows it is too generous, and "corrects everything under it" in N4 is U5 wearing U1's clothes.

### U6. Unity as the domain over which psychological traction is non-additive

*Formulation.* A system is one mind over the largest set of parts S such that psychology's explanation of S is not the sum of its explanations of S's members. MON:104 states the test for institutions: traction "shows when institutional psychology predicts what the psychology of the members, taken one at a time, does not." MbD:99 names the signature: unity "generates the capacity and interference effects — Stroop, dual-task costs, binding across domains: regularities that exist only because processing transpires within one economy."

*Hard cases.* Split-brain: the patient's psychology is not the sum of two hemispheric psychologies (cross-cueing, unified action) and is not a normal single psychology either. Non-additivity is partial, and measurable by which interference effects survive. Monastery: Ocasio's and Weick and Roberts's attributions (MON:104) are claims of non-additivity, and the verdict is MON:106's graded one. Nation: polls sum; political psychology is largely additive; no unity, agreeing with MON:164. MoE: no expert has a psychology, so the model's psychology is non-additive trivially; unified. Swarm: unified to the degree that swarm-level regularities exist that no agent shows; an open empirical question. DID: Huntjens's transfers show that the person's psychology is not the sum of the identities' (DIS), so one mind with partitioned access. Forward pass versus session: a session's behavior is not the sum of per-pass behaviors, since in-context learning is a session-level regularity; U6 grants the session unity for mindedness while N2 denies it a field. That is exactly the split between mindedness and experience the theory wants, and no other candidate delivers it as cleanly.

*Graded.* Yes, by the size of the interaction terms, like any non-additivity.

*Interaction.* With N2: independent, which is a virtue. The unity of a mind and the unity of a field are then two conditions that can hold apart. With N5: a subject's share in an occurrence is its share in the traction.

*Recommendation.* Adopt as the *criterion*, with U3 and U4 as the *mechanism* and U2 as the *scale measure*. U6 is the unity condition that MbD's grounding implies, since MbD §2 grounds the kind in psychology's success and MbD:99 already maps unity to non-additive effects. Its weakness is that it is an epistemic test and names no mechanism, which is why it needs U3 and U4: a unified mind is the domain of non-additive traction, sustained by vehicles in a shared format within one economy of error. That replaces N4's unity row, repairs MbD:59's "single economy" (now: one economy of error, in one currency), and keeps MON's nation verdict.

## What the proposal most needs before it could frame the mindedness essay

1. **Add the fifth feature to N1:** the reference is revised in place, at runtime, by the vehicles it corrects. Without it the persona transformer and the cerebellum both pass, and N3's across-token verdict falls. This is one sentence and it carries (c), (f) and the gate test.
2. **Define admission as being a term of the joint constraint, not a target of its enforcement.** Without it the suppressed face is experienced. This also gives the field a definition that does not reduce to "whatever the reference touches."
3. **Replace N4's reduction with a mapping that admits what does not reduce.** Simulation is mirroring, not decoupling; accuracy is fit to targets, not to the reference; multi-level content is content determinacy, not control grain. State the architecture as what unity and experience share, and leave the other three as conditions on the vehicles and on the direction of revision.
4. **Replace the unity row with U6 as criterion, U3 plus U4 as mechanism, U2 as scale.** This is the only combination that keeps the nation verdict, grants a session mindedness without a field, and gives MbD:99's interference effects their place.
5. **Specify the balance in (d) as a ratio of gains with two failure modes,** and name the quiet one. The proposal's novelty is the direction of correction; a theory of direction that describes only one direction's failure is half a theory.
6. **Measure lifetimes with the loop open,** by the playback intervention of ESOM:77, and restate MON:130's count as a ratio of two relaxation times.
7. **Take the first horn on rivalry:** it is the paradigm of defense and a minimal field, not the paradigm of experience; the joint constraint that makes a field is projected from a model. That gives "experience requires mindedness" its mechanism and the fragment its exclusion.
8. **Restate the diagnosis fairly:** ESOM:65 had a reference; the proposal adds its direction and its persistence. The essay will be stronger for claiming less.
9. **Drop the composer analogy** and say that belonging is partial constitution with no presence in it; then decide whether the monastery is a subject of experience in the same sense in which it is a subject of a mind.
10. **Add one overflow test to §6,** since none of the six separates the proposal from global workspace theory with feedback.

N6 and N7 can wait for the personhood and ethics essays. Neither bears on the mindedness essay's frame.
