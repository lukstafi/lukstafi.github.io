The human-side prompts that developed and steered the draft, in conversation order. Assistant responses, button replies, and interface metadata are omitted.

The question and its scope

We probably need to address https://www.apronus.com/philosophy/dialogues/monastery-conscious and the Schwitzgebel paper, does it make more sense stand-alone or as part of a mindedness centered essay that we thought would follow the two AI deep dives? Three options in total: (1) a dedicated essay that jumps the queue, (2) part of mindedness essay and it waits its turn, (3) pull mindedness forward. Note: the USA and the monastery are distinct examples due to scales involved. For the monastery, it’s easy to see that any phenomenal experiences that are for it are precisely the phenomenal experiences that are for the monks; and the question then is what’s the phenomenal and metaphysical status of any additional phenomenal field.

However, note that mindedness is important, since for the monastery it’s the bigger jump than from mindedness to phenomenality.

For the USA, there’s an argument from residual integration: on the organizational ladder, all relevant integrations are discharged before reaching the level of USA, so we can conclude about USA even when group-human-mind is not resolved.

Length: 6000 with room to grow to 8000 words. Important: consider if the simulation criterion / condition for mindedness is the harder one for groups to meet.
