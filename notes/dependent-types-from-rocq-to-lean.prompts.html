Initial request

For an upcoming article for our blog, let’s do a deep dive into dependent type systems, in particular calculus of constructions, as in Rocq, and the system behind Lean, its evolution, and Lean4. Inspired by this article https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/zHhGa3iQBQcdSifFL/a-critical-look-at-lean4-1 But the motivation is to refresh my forgotten understanding of Rocq (Coq at the time) and use it to teach me Lean. A fruitful triangulation for the level of writing is:

  • People who used to be familiar with dependent types at a rather deep level but in their past life and need a refresher
  • People who are unfamiliar with dependent types but are proficient mathematicians
