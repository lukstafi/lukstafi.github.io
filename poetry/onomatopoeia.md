onomatopoeia

some words -- call out:  
round, loud  
or screeching traction  
or precise, pertinent  
or subtle, evanescent  
or in the end  
awe

calls coming to shake and point  
in the direction  
like swallowing a throw

sounds only hiss  
shushing  
isn't silence
