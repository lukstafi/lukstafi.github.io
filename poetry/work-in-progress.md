work in progress

Tap two tears by the riverbed,  
unabating muscle.  
Frame the waters like mountain lakes  
as clear as the limbs.  
Inbreathe a tightrope of the sky,  
the laughing goddess.  
Hold firm the moment  
and another, and another.
