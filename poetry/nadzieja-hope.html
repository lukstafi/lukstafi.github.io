nadzieja / hope

2010-03-02

nadzieja

podziwiam
jak do mnie machasz
zza brzegu wyobraźni

---
--- translated Sep 12, 2020:

hope

I admire
you reaching out
from beyond the imagined

--- literal translation Nov 7, 2020:

hope

I admire
how you wave to me
from beyond the shores of imagination
