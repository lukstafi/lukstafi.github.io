raptor

winged face  
fasting's made you sharp  
senses tidied up  
when you swoop without regard  
to unfriendly ground  
thickened blood  
the life that you hunt  
the smell keeps you awake  
wide above the plain  
by an untold spell  
you soar  
a falcon a hawk
