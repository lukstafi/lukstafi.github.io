cisza / silence

2010-09-21

***

cisza --
polna mysza
umywa uszy
i szyje dalej

---
[translated: June 16, 2011]

***

silence!
harvest mice
stop to listen
then sail away

---

[update: May 30, 2019]

Originally I used "sew away" above, to match the Polish version semantically.
